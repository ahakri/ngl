# High-demand, low-supply candidates: result of the red-team selection

27 November 2026. Eight candidate data businesses were each given a quantified case (buyers, price anchors, every competitor found, revenue arithmetic) and scored 1–10 against four tests. Two further wildcard candidates and seven of the eight red-team passes did not run because the account's usage limit was reached. Figures rest on web-search summaries; pages could not be opened.

**Result: no candidate meets the bar.** Every one fails on supply. In each market, small self-serve competitors or free tools already exist at the prices a solo product would charge.

| Candidate | Demand | Monetisation | Scarcity | Solo feasibility | Direct competitors found |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Grant prospecting from IRS 990 data | 7 | 5 | 3 | 8 | 8, incl. free Grantmakers.io and Candid's free tier |
| Healthcare sales prospecting from federal data | 6 | 5 | 3 | 8 | 9, from $199/month |
| AI-recommendation benchmark (law firms) | 6 | 5 | 3 | 7 | 3 small, plus a crowded general category |
| Planning-stage construction leads | 6 | 5 | 3 | 6 | 10, from $29/month per county |
| Keyword alerts across local governments | 6 | 5 | 3 | 6 | 18, from $6/month, one free |
| Insurer payment-rate benchmarks for small practices | 6 | 4 | 2 | 6 | 11, from free to $99/month |
| Franchise disclosure data | 6 | 4 | 2 | 7 | 13, four of them free |
| Home and auto insurance rate filings | 5 | 4 | 3 | 5 | 12, three of them free |

Scales: demand 7 = tens of thousands of buyers; monetisation 5 = about $100k a year within 24 months, 10 = $1M+; scarcity 3–4 = several affordable competitors exist, 7 = enterprise-only.

## What this means

- The typical outcome across all eight is about $100k–250k a year after two years, with $1M a year a four-to-five-year stretch at best.
- Markets with many buyers and public source data attract small entrants quickly; several of these competitors launched in 2024–2026.
- The one red-team pass that ran (grant prospecting) returned "killed", although it could not run fresh searches.
- The data-centre siting tracker already built scored lowest on demand but had the least cheap direct competition of anything examined.

## Implication

A data product that is high on demand, monetisation and scarcity at once, and buildable by one person from public sources, was not found. What separates the candidates is not the niche but who can reach the buyers: distribution, not data.
