# Options for one student developer: low upfront cost, best realistic return

27 November 2026. Re-ranks the routes on what matters for a solo student: cash needed up front, hours, time to first dollar, and whether buyers can be reached without a budget or network. Evidence is noted per row; where a row says "judgement" it was not researched and is an informed estimate only.

| Route | Upfront cost | Time to first dollar | Who brings the buyers | Realistic first-year return | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Freelance data, scraping and automation work | $0 | Days to weeks | Freelance marketplaces | Scales with hours worked; the most dependable | Judgement |
| Collectors sold through a marketplace (e.g. Apify) | $0 | Weeks | The marketplace (takes ~20%) | Usually small; a few earn well | Apify reported paying developers $563k in Sept 2025 and says top creators pass $10k/month, naming none |
| Siteline: free weekly digest, then paid alerts | Hosting, about $5–10/month | Months | You, via posts and the digest | Most likely near zero; a minority chance of a few thousand a month | Built and running on real data; no buyer asked yet |
| Game-development skills (Roblox, Blender): commissions, assets, tools | $0 | Weeks | Existing creator communities | Varies widely | Roblox paid creators $1.7B in the year to June 2026, but the median was about $1,500 |
| A new niche subscription data product | Low | Many months | You, by selling | Tens of thousands a year at best | Ten candidates researched and red-teamed; all killed |

## Reading the table

- **Nothing here is both low-cost and high-return with good odds.** Low cost makes it cheap to try several routes; it does not raise the odds on any one.
- **The dependable money is in selling skills, not products.** Freelance work pays from the first job and needs no audience.
- **Product routes are lottery tickets with cheap entry.** Worth running in parallel as experiments with a fixed time limit, not as the main plan.
- **Marketplaces solve the hardest part for a student**, which is reaching buyers. Their weakness is crowding: most niches already hold several cheap listings.

## Suggested plan

1. Earn now from freelance data and automation work; the Siteline code is a credible portfolio piece.
2. Run Siteline as a four-week experiment: publish a free weekly digest of data-centre moratoria and hearings, and count sign-ups. Stop if nobody subscribes.
3. Package one or two collectors for a marketplace only where a search shows fewer than three existing listings.

## Open questions that would change the ranking

- Hours available each week.
- Any existing audience (Discord, YouTube, a developer community).
- Whether the game-development route is acceptable; it was excluded earlier in this project.
