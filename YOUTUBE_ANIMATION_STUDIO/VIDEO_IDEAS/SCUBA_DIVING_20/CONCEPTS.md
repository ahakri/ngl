## SCUBA-01 — He Lived 60 Hours at the Bottom of the Sea
*Alt title:* The Cook Who Breathed in a Sunken Ship  
*Score:* **84/100** (demand 17, story 18, packaging 17, retention 16, feasibility 16) · *Difficulty:* Medium · *Duration:* 62 s

**Subject:** Harrison Okene, cook on the tug Jascon-4, capsized off Nigeria on 26 May 2013. He survived ~60 h in an air pocket (~4 ft high) at ~30 m until DCN divers, sent to recover bodies, found him on 28 May. He then spent ~60 h in decompression. 11 crew died.

**Why it can work:** It has a single impossible number (60 hours), a shrinking-air clock, and a twist (the divers were recovering bodies). The air-pocket physics suits animation. The real rescue footage went viral globally in Dec 2013, so recognition is proven. It is evergreen survival content.

**Verification:** VERIFIED this session (ABC, SCMP, Global News, My Modern Met). Depth reports vary (~30 m / ~100 ft); use '~30 m'.

**15-second hook:**

- 0-2 s: Upside-down tug interior, one torch, waterline at chin. VO: 'This man spent 60 hours at the bottom of the Atlantic, and lived.'
- 2-6 s: Map pin, Nigeria, 26 May 2013, '30 m'. Hull rolls in silhouette.
- 6-15 s: HUD 'AIR POCKET: 4 FT', hours counter starts. VO: 'He was the cook. Eleven others didn't make it. And the divers who finally came weren't looking for survivors.'

**Structure:**

1. Cold open: impossible fact + inverted cabin
2. Capsize at ~04:50 in heavy swell (reported) - he is in the toilet compartment
3. Feeling through dark corridors to an air pocket
4. Clock: cold, darkness, sea water rising, CO2 building (explained by callout)
5. Mid-turn: dive team arrives to recover bodies
6. The hand: a diver's hand is grabbed (shown gently, stylised)
7. Mechanism: why he couldn't just swim up - decompression; 60 h in a chamber
8. Loop: 'the diver thought the hand was a body, until it squeezed back'

**Retention moments:** 0 s impossible-fact line; 6 s hours counter starts; 25 s 'weren't looking for survivors' twist; 38 s hand-grab reveal; 48 s decompression mechanism cutaway; 58 s loop line

**Thumbnail / first frame:** Inverted cabin cross-section; tiny figure in a bright air bubble at top, black water below, '60 HOURS' in yellow.

**Animation style:** Blender low-poly cross-section of a capsized tug, volumetric torch beam, rising waterline shader, HUD counters, cool teal/black palette.

**Scenes:** Exterior tug capsizing in storm (silhouette); Inverted interior corridor with floating debris; Air pocket close-up with waterline; Seabed exterior with diver lights approaching; Diver POV through hatch; Decompression chamber 2.5D diagram; Map/date card
