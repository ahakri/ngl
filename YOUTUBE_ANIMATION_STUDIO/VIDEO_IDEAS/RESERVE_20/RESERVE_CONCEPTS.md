### SCUBA-R1 — They Drifted to Dragon Island
*Alt title:* Lost at Sea, Then a Komodo Dragon · *Score:* **80/100** (D15 S17 P17 R16 F15) · *Difficulty:* Medium · *Duration:* 58 s

**Subject:** June 2008, Komodo National Park, Indonesia: five divers (three British, one French, one Swedish) were swept away by strong currents off Tatawa island. They drifted for more than 12 hours, about 20 miles, and washed ashore on remote Rinca island, where a Komodo dragon repeatedly approached. They drove it off with rocks and driftwood. A search with ~30 boats found them the next day; they were dehydrated but had no serious injuries. Reported ordeal length varies (~36 h to 2 days; NBC's 'five days' is an outlier).

**Why it can work:** A double-threat survival story: open-water drift (like the Lonergans, but they lived) then a prehistoric predator. The twist lands mid-video and the outcome is positive. It is a natural Short with a loopable first frame (heads in the sea, then a dragon on the beach).

**Verification:** VERIFIED (CBS, NBC, Jakarta Post, Dawn, VOA). Ordeal duration varies (36 h to 2 days) - say 'more than a day'. Do not invent names or dialogue.

**15-second hook:**

- 0-2 s: Five tiny heads in a swirling current, the dive boat gone. VO: 'Five divers surfaced and their boat was gone.'
- 2-6 s: 'Komodo National Park, 2008'. Drift-line map.
- 6-15 s: VO: 'They drifted all night to an island. Then something came out of the trees.' A dragon silhouette on the sand.

**Structure:**

1. Hook: empty sea
2. Komodo's notorious currents (map arrows)

4. Landfall on Rinca island
5. Twist: Komodo dragon approaches; rocks and driftwood
