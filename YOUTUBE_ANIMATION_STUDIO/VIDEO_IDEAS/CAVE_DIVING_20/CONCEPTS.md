## CAVE-01 — He Went 270 m Down to Bring a Body Home

- **Alt title:** The Promise That Killed Dave Shaw
- **Category:** Cave diving — fatal accident / body recovery  |  **Duration:** 62 s  |  **Difficulty:** Medium
- **Scores:** demand 16, story 18, packaging 17, retention 16, feasibility 15 → **82/100**

**Subject.** Boesmansgat (Bushman's Hole), Northern Cape, South Africa. 8 Jan 2005: Dave Shaw died at ~270 m trying to recover the body of Deon Dreyer (died Dec 1994). Support diver Don Shirley was injured on his own dive and survived. Dreyer's body surfaced days later tangled in Shaw's line.

**Why it has potential.** Combines the niche's two strongest triggers: body recovery (Dive Talk 9.15M) and an extreme number (270 m, ~15-min descent vs ~12-h ascent). Covered repeatedly in long-form (MrBallen, doc 'Dave Not Coming Back') and in Shorts, including non-English ones, so demand travels. Few 3D-animated Shorts exist. A depth cross-section is ideal for our pipeline.

**Hook (0–15 s).** 0–2 s: Torch beam hits a still diver shape on the cave floor, depth readout 270 M. VO: 'He found the body at 270 metres. Then it pulled him down.' 2–6 s: Cross-section snaps in: surface far above, a 12-HOUR ASCENT bar. VO: 'Getting down took 15 minutes. Getting back up would take 12 hours.' 6–15 s: Sinkhole in desert scrub at dawn. VO: 'This is Bushman's Hole, South Africa, and this is why Dave Shaw never came back. (reported)'

**Structure.**
1. Cold open: torch on body at 270 m
2. Complication: 15 min down vs 12 h up
3. Setup: Oct 2004, Shaw finds 20-yr-old Deon Dreyer, lost here in Dec 1994
4. The plan: return Jan 2005 to bring him home for his family
5. Descent: dot drops through the cross-section, line reel, Don Shirley staging below
6. The failure: body is heavier than planned, lines tangle, breathing becomes laboured (later attributed to CO2 build-up, as reported)
7. Consequence: Shaw does not ascend; Shirley injured on his own dive
8. Payoff: days later both bodies rise together; Dreyer comes home
9. Loop/hard cut: the still surface of the sinkhole

**Retention moments.** Depth counter ticking past 100/200/270 m; Twist at ~22 s: he'd already found the body once before; Tension pause before 'the line wrapped around him'; Reveal: the body he came for rose with him

**Thumbnail / cover frame.** Tiny diver silhouette at the bottom of a vast black cross-section, one torch beam, red '270 M' and thin line back up to a pin-prick of light.

**Animation style.** 3D low-poly diver in a volumetric-fog sinkhole + 2.5D cross-section with a depth bar; cool cyan palette; no faces.

**Scenes.** Desert sinkhole exterior at dawn (procedural terrain + water plane); Vertical shaft descent with bubbles and particles; Cave floor at depth: silt, torch cone, sheathed body shape (abstract, wrapped, no gore); Cross-section diagram with diver dots and a 12-h deco bar; Tangled line close-up (curve objects); Surface: two shapes rising (silhouette), sunrise

VO: 'The only way home was hours of diving, and none of them could dive.' 6–15 s: Rain on the cave mouth, 23 June caption. VO: 'Thailand, 2018. This is how 13 people came out of Tham Luang.'
