## CAVE-01 — He Went 270 m Down to Bring a Body Home

- **Alt title:** The Promise That Killed Dave Shaw
- **Category:** Cave diving — fatal accident / body recovery  |  **Duration:** 62 s  |  **Difficulty:** Medium
- **Rank:** 1  |  **Scores:** demand 16, story 18, packaging 17, retention 16, feasibility 15 → **82/100**

**Subject.** Boesmansgat (Bushman's Hole), Northern Cape, South Africa. Oct 2004: Dave Shaw found the body of Deon Dreyer (died Dec 1994) at ~270 m (Wikipedia gives 272 m). 8 Jan 2005: Shaw died trying to recover it. Support diver Don Shirley went down to ~250 m to look for him, got decompression sickness and was left with permanent balance damage. On 12 Jan 2005, while Shaw's equipment was being recovered, both bodies floated up close to the surface and were recovered.

**Why it has potential.** Combines the niche's two strongest triggers: body recovery (Dive Talk 9.15M) and an extreme number (270 m, ~15-min descent vs ~12-h ascent). Covered repeatedly in long-form (MrBallen, doc 'Dave Not Coming Back') and in Shorts, including non-English ones, so demand travels. Few 3D-animated Shorts exist. A depth cross-section is ideal for our pipeline.

**Hook (0–15 s).** 0–2 s: Torch beam hits a still diver shape on the cave floor, depth readout 270 M. VO: 'He found the body at 270 metres. He never came back up.' 2–6 s: Cross-section snaps in: surface far above, a 12-HOUR ASCENT bar. VO: 'Getting down took 15 minutes. Getting back up would take 12 hours.' 6–15 s: Sinkhole in desert scrub at dawn. VO: 'This is Bushman's Hole, South Africa, and this is why Dave Shaw never came back. (reported)'

**Structure.**
1. Cold open: torch on body at 270 m
2. Complication: 15 min down vs 12 h up
3. Setup: Oct 2004, Shaw finds 20-yr-old Deon Dreyer, lost here in Dec 1994
4. The plan: return Jan 2005 to bring him home for his family
5. Descent: dot drops through the cross-section, line reel, Don Shirley staging below
6. The failure: while handling the body his line tangles and his breathing labours (CO2 build-up, as reported by Divernet)
7. Consequence: Shaw does not ascend; Shirley injured on his own dive
8. Payoff: days later both bodies rise together; Dreyer comes home
9. Loop/hard cut: the still surface of the sinkhole

**Retention moments.** Depth counter ticking past 100/200/270 m; Twist at ~22 s: he'd already found the body once before; Tension pause before 'the line wrapped around him'; Reveal: the body he came for rose with him

**Thumbnail / cover frame.** Tiny diver silhouette at the bottom of a vast black cross-section, one torch beam, red '270 M' and thin line back up to a pin-prick of light.

**Animation style.** 3D low-poly diver in a volumetric-fog sinkhole + 2.5D cross-section with a depth bar; cool cyan palette; no faces.

**Scenes.** Desert sinkhole exterior at dawn (procedural terrain + water plane); Vertical shaft descent with bubbles and particles; Cave floor at depth: silt, torch cone, sheathed body shape (abstract, wrapped, no gore); Cross-section diagram with diver dots and a 12-h deco bar; Tangled line close-up (curve objects); Surface: two shapes rising (silhouette), sunrise

## CAVE-03 — Under the Ice, the Only Way Out Was Deeper

Huotarinen died shortly after the deepest section (~110 m below the entrance level). Uusimäki also died; police believe he panicked on finding Huotarinen, while Känkänen tried to help him (reported). The three survivors exited after long decompression.

**Why it has potential.** Ice entry, a deep traverse with no easy way back, and a survivor forced to pass a body make a vivid story. Multiple long-form and Short versions exist, plus a feature documentary (2016). Plura's dive centre has complained that YouTube is full of misleading Plura videos (divers24.com), so an accurate, careful version can stand out.

VO: 'They cut a hole in the ice and dived into a mountain.' 2–6 s: Cross-section: entry, a dip to depth, exit far away. VO: 'The way out was through the deepest part of the cave.' 6–15 s: Five lights descending. VO: 'Norway, February 2014. Five went in. This is what happened to the other two.'

**Structure.**
1. Cold open: ice triangle
2. Stakes: traverse, deep middle, cold
3. Team of five, months of planning (no motives invented)
4. Descent: the constriction at depth
5. Failure: one diver gets into trouble at depth; a second also dies (exact sequence reported differently; keep it to agreed facts)
6. Survivors must continue past/around a body to exit
7. Hours of decompression in near-freezing water
8. Payoff: authorities call recovery too dangerous (link to Part 2: friends return)

**Retention moments.** Ice-cut visual; Depth bar dipping then rising (U-shaped route); 'Two did not' reveal at ~30 s; Series hook: 'so their friends went back'

**Thumbnail / cover frame.** Triangle of light in black ice seen from below, five torch beams; text 'ONLY WAY OUT'.

**Animation style.** 3D under-ice scene with caustics + U-shaped 2.5D cross-section; cold blue/white palette.

**Scenes.** Snowy valley and frozen river; Ice-hole from below with light shafts; Narrow deep restriction; U-shaped cross-section route; Deco stop: lone diver holding a line, timer; Surface: snow, three figures

## CAVE-06 — It Looks Like a Pond. It's Killed More Than 10 Divers.

- **Alt title:** The Mount Everest of Cave Diving
- **Category:** Cave diving — deadly site  |  **Duration:** 55 s  |  **Difficulty:** Low
- **Rank:** 6  |  **Scores:** demand 15, story 13, packaging 15, retention 14, feasibility 16 → **73/100**

**Subject.** Eagle's Nest sink, Chassahowitzka Wildlife Management Area near Weeki Wachee, Hernando County, Florida, USA. Nicknamed 'the Mount Everest of cave diving'. At least 10 deaths since 1981 (National Geographic; AOL says at least 13). Closed to diving 1999–2003. In October 2016 two experienced divers died there while a third friend stayed nearer the surface.

**Why it has potential.** Same template as The Shaft (MrBallen 7.1M): an ordinary-looking pond above a giant deep chamber. It's an iconic, frequently searched site. A place-profile works well as a Short and could be serialised ('Deadliest caves #1').

**Hook (0–15 s).** 0–2 s: Overhead of a small duckweed-covered pond in woods. VO: 'This pond has killed more than ten divers.' 2–6 s: Camera sinks below the surface into a massive dark chamber. VO: 'Under the surface, it opens into a room the size of a cathedral.' 6–15 s: Depth bar dropping. VO: 'Florida's Eagle's Nest. Divers call it the Mount Everest of cave diving.'

**Structure.**
1. Cold open: innocent pond
