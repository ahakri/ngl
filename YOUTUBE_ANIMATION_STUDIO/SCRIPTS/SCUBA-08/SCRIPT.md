## Clean read (for TTS / SRT)

This ship sank in 1915. You can still read its name.
It's Ernest Shackleton's Endurance, three kilometres under the Weddell Sea.
Pack ice trapped it for months, then crushed it.
It sank, but all 28 men on board survived.
The ship wasn't seen again for over a century.
A 2019 search lost its underwater robot and was called off.
In 2022, a new expedition went back, aboard an icebreaker.
Its underwater drone searched the seabed, three kilometres down.
On March 5th, it found her.
About four miles from the position her captain had recorded.
Upright, intact, 3,008 metres down.
Experts say the creatures that eat wooden wrecks elsewhere don't live in these waters.
Today it's a protected monument. Nothing may be taken.
And it was found 100 years to the day after Shackleton's funeral.

**Loop line:** And it was found 100 years to the day after Shackleton's funeral.
