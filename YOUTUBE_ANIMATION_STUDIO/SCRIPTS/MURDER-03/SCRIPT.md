# MURDER-03: He Hijacked a Plane, Then Jumped Into the Night

- **Case:** "D.B. Cooper", the man who bought a ticket as "Dan Cooper" and hijacked Northwest Orient Flight 305 (Boeing 727, Portland to Seattle) on 24 Nov 1971. He jumped from the aft airstair over SW Washington. About $5,800 of the ransom was found at Tena Bar on the Columbia River on 10 Feb 1980. The FBI ended active investigation in July 2016.
- **Format:** YouTube Short, 9:16, murder/mystery band (55–80 s). Series: *Cold File*.
- **Voice:** Kokoro `am_onyx`, speed 1.06 (test render). **200 words, 74.1 s of measured narration.** Target 70–75 s.
- **Machine file:** `script.json` (same folder). Storyboard: `STORYBOARDS/MURDER-03/STORYBOARD.md`. Fact-check: `QUALITY_CONTROL/MURDER-03/FACTCHECK.md`.
- **Tone rules applied:**
  - No suspects named; the FBI never charged anyone.
  - No invented dialogue. The note is paraphrased only.
  - The finder is not named.
  - Disputed geography is labelled DISPUTED, and the money theories are labelled THEORY.
  - No one was harmed in this case, so there is no gore issue. The hijacker is a faceless silhouette, with no composite sketch.
