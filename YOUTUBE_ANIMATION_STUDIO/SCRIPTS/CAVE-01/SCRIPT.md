## Clean read (for TTS / SRT)

He found a body 270 metres down.
Then he went back for it.
Ten minutes down. About ten hours of stops back up.
This is Boesmansgat, Bushman's Hole, South Africa.
In 2004, Australian pilot Dave Shaw set a depth record here.
At the bottom, his torch found another diver.
Deon Dreyer. Lost here in 1994, never recovered.
Shaw planned one more dive, to bring him home.
January 2005. Don Shirley would meet him at 220 metres.
Shaw reached the body and began easing it into a bag.
This deep, dense gas makes every breath hard work.
His lines snagged. He worked harder. Carbon dioxide built up.
He never reached 220.
Shirley searched deeper, and was struck by decompression sickness. He survived.
Shaw's helmet camera recorded it all.
Experts concluded carbon dioxide made him black out.
Four days later, divers hauled up his lines.
Two bodies came up, tangled together.
Ten years after he was lost, Deon Dreyer came home.

**Loop line:** Ten years after he was lost, Deon Dreyer came home.
