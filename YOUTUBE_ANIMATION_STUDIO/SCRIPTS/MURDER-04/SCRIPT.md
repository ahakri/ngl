# MURDER-04: The Zodiac's Cipher Was Cracked After 51 Years

- **Case:** the Zodiac Killer's 340-symbol cipher, mailed to the San Francisco Chronicle in November 1969. It was solved in December 2020 by David Oranchak (USA), Sam Blake (Australia) and Jarl Van Eycke (Belgium), using AZdecrypt. The FBI San Francisco office acknowledged the solution on 11 Dec 2020. The killer has never been identified.
- **Format:** YouTube Short, 9:16, murder/mystery band (55–80 s). Series: *Cold File*.
- **Voice:** Kokoro `am_michael`, speed 1.08 (test render). **192 words, 75.8 s of measured narration** (about 152 wpm including pauses). Target 70–76 s.
- **Machine file:** `script.json` (same folder). Storyboard: `STORYBOARDS/MURDER-04/STORYBOARD.md`. Fact-check: `QUALITY_CONTROL/MURDER-04/FACTCHECK.md`.
- **⚠ Fact-check status: PROVISIONAL.** The web-search budget ran out before the MURDER-04 searches. Claims rest on the concepts.json research sources and need a live re-check (8 queued queries, each with fallback wording) **before render**. See FACTCHECK.md.
- **Tone rules applied:**
  - The story is about the codebreakers. Violence stays off-screen: one respectful line, no locations of deaths, no victim names.
  - Only the first two non-graphic plaintext lines are quoted.
  - No suspect is named.
  - The "TV show" interpretation is labelled THEORY.
  - The glyphs are illustrative stand-ins.
  - A memorial stamp appears on the last frame.
