## Clean read (for TTS / SRT)

Air for one diver to get out. There were two.
So one stayed behind, inside the rock.
Mallorca, Spain. April 2017. The cave of Sa Piqueta.
Xisco Gràcia and Guillem Mascaró were mapping it.
Their guideline broke. Their gas ran low.
Gràcia gave his partner his remaining gas and sent him for help.
Then he waited alone in an air pocket, about 900 metres in.
Fresh water to drink. Almost no light. Air thick with carbon dioxide.
Hours went by. He began to believe his partner hadn't made it.
Above him, rescuers tried to drill down through the rock. It failed.
Divers tried to swim in. The water was too murky.
The bad air made him hallucinate.
Then, around hour 60, a light rose through the water.
It was real.
Rescue divers had found him. Alive.
His partner had made it out, and raised the alarm.
Air for one diver to get out. Both came home.

**Loop line:** Air for one diver to get out. Both came home.
