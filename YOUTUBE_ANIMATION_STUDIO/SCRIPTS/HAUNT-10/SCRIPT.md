## Clean read (for TTS / SRT)

This staircase climbs straight into the ceiling.
Legend says it was built to confuse ghosts.
Winchester Mystery House, San Jose, California.
Rifle heiress Sarah Winchester bought a farmhouse here.
She kept adding to it for decades, until she died in 1922.
A medium supposedly warned her about the ghosts of Winchester rifle victims.
To escape them, the story goes, she had to keep building forever.
In 1906, an earthquake cut the house from seven floors to four.
And the stairs into the ceiling? Likely leftovers from remodeling.
Her biographer, Mary Jo Ignoffo, found no evidence for the ghost story.
Soon after she died, the house opened to paying visitors.
The ghost story sold the tickets.
Whatever the truth, one thing hasn't changed.
