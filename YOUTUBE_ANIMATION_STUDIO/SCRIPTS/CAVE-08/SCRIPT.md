## Clean read (for TTS / SRT)

A diver stayed missing under this swimming hole for 21 years.
Thirty feet down, the well turns into a flooded cave.
The water wells up from an aquifer below.
This is Jacob's Well, in Wimberley, Texas.
Its chambers drop more than a hundred feet.
Divers have died in them. Nobody even agrees how many.
In 1979, two young divers went in together.
Neither came back up.
Deeper in, the passages squeeze down tight.
And the floor is loose gravel that can slide.
Recovery divers went in after them, but couldn't bring them out.
One of the recovery divers then built a grate to keep others out.
In 1981, a flood carried one of the young divers back out.
The other stayed lost.
For 21 years.
In 2000, a recovery team pushed into the fourth chamber.
And there, at last, they found him.
Today, scuba diving here is banned, except for one exploration team.
And from up top, it still looks like a swimming hole.

**Loop line:** And from up top, it still looks like a swimming hole.

## Sources (key → URL → what it supports)

- **J1** — https://iucrr.org/?p=415 (International Underwater Cave Rescue and Recovery incident record, search snippets) — Accident 9 Sep 1979 at Jacob's Well (Trinity Aquifer, Hays County, Cypress Creek); 'Victim #1 was recovered 21 years after he was reported missing'; recovery by San Marcos Area Recovery Team (S.M.A.R.T.) divers starting 11 Oct 2000
- **J2** — https://visitwimberley.com/jacobswell/lBond/2.shtml (+ /3.shtml, /4.shtml; reprinted at https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/135287131/richard-madison-patton) — Louie Bond, 'The Fatal Allure of Jacob's Well': Pasadena divers Kent Maupin and Mark Brashier; a 1979 recovery attempt failed in the sliding gravel of the third chamber (the diver was rescued by other divers); Jan 1980 grate of rebar and concrete; Brashier's remains washed out in a 1981 flood; 2000 SMART dive found traces of Maupin in the fourth chamber, >100 ft below the surface; tight passage to the fourth chamber; unstable gravel floor in the third
- **J3** — https://grunge.com/191692/the-dangerous-truth-of-jacobs-well (Grunge; search snippets) — 9 Sep 1979: Kent Maupin and Mark Brashier dived in and never resurfaced; one diver's remains flushed out of the well in 1981, the other's not recovered until 2000; the recovery diver later installed a grate to keep people out; death-toll estimates vary
