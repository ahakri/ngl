## Clean read (for TTS / SRT)

This lump came off a 2,000-year-old shipwreck.
Inside, there were gears.
In 1900, Greek sponge divers sheltered from a storm off Antikythera.
Around 50 metres down, they found a wreck full of statues.
They worked in brass helmets, breathing air pumped down from the boat.
The salvage ran into 1901.
One diver died of the bends. Two were paralysed.
Among the bronze and marble came this corroded lump.
In 1902, it split apart, and a gear wheel showed through.
In 2005, X-ray CT scans revealed hidden gears and inscriptions.
It was a geared calculator for the sky.
It tracked the Sun and Moon, and predicted eclipses.
Nothing this complex appears again for over a thousand years.
And the men who found it were only looking for sponges.

**Loop line:** And the men who found it were only looking for sponges.
