## Clean read (for TTS / SRT)

A driver says he hit a girl on this hill.
Then she vanished.
Blue Bell Hill, Kent, on the A229 near Maidstone.
In 1974, a local bricklayer reported hitting a young girl here at night.
He covered her with a blanket and went for the police.
When he came back with officers, only the blanket was there.
And he wasn't the last.
In November 1992, motorists again reported running down a young woman here.
No trace of her was ever found.
Sceptics call it a classic folk tale, told on roads worldwide.
Even local accounts disagree on when the sightings began.
None of these reports has ever been confirmed.
So if someone steps into your headlights on this hill...
