## Clean read (for TTS / SRT)

He was stuck head-down, hundreds of feet inside a cave.
He couldn't turn around. He couldn't back out.
The rescuers' plan: pull him back up the way he came.
Nutty Putty Cave, Utah. November 24th, 2009.
John Edward Jones was 26.
Deep in the cave, he squeezed head-first into a narrow passage.
It ran downward. He slid in until he was wedged, upside down.
Rescuers rigged ropes and pulleys, anchored into the rock.
At first, it seemed to work.
For the first time in hours, there was hope.
Then, reports say, an anchor gave way.
He slid straight back into the crack.
Rescuers kept working, hour after hour.
After more than 27 hours, John Jones died in the cave.
Officials judged recovering him too dangerous.
So the cave was sealed with concrete, with him still inside.
Nutty Putty Cave is now his resting place.
