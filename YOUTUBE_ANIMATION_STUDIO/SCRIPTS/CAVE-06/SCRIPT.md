## Clean read (for TTS / SRT)

This pond has killed at least ten divers.
From above, it's just a pond in the woods.
Below, a narrow shaft called the Chimney drops into the dark.
Then it opens into a huge room divers call the Ballroom.
Florida's Eagle's Nest. Divers call it the Mount Everest of cave diving.
The cave runs on past the Ballroom, to about 300 feet deep.
120 feet down, there's a sign with the Grim Reaper on it.
It reads: Stop. Prevent your death. Go no farther.
In 1999, partly over safety fears, it was closed to divers.
Cave divers pushed back. It reopened in 2003.
October 2016. Three friends came to dive it.
One stayed near the surface. Two experienced divers went deep.
They were due back within an hour. They never surfaced.
Recovery divers found them the next morning, around 260 feet down.
Exactly what went wrong is still unclear.
And from above, it still looks like a pond.

**Loop line:** And from above, it still looks like a pond.
