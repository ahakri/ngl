## Clean read (for TTS / SRT)

They cut a hole in the ice and swam into a mountain.
The exit: two kilometres away, past a squeeze over 100 metres down.
Norway, February 2014. Five Finnish divers went in.
Three came out.
The cave is called Plura, in northern Norway.
The plan: swim right through, and climb out in another cave.
Near-freezing water. Two teams, two hours apart.
Team one reached the squeeze.
The first diver slipped through. Then he looked back.
His buddy's light was signalling. He was stuck, tangled in the passage.
His buddy never made it through.
Then team two arrived.
In the deep section, a diver from team two also died.
The man behind him had no way forward.
So he turned around, and swam all the way back.
Hours of decompression later, all three survivors were in hospital.
The official recovery failed. Police banned diving in the cave.
Weeks later, the survivors went back anyway, to bring their friends home.
