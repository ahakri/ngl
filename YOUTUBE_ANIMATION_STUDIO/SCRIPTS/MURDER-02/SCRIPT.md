# MURDER-02 — The Dead Man With 'Tamam Shud' in a Hidden Pocket

- **Case:** Somerton Man / Tamam Shud case — man found dead on Somerton Beach, Adelaide, 1 Dec 1948; exhumed May 2021; proposed in July 2022 by Prof. Derek Abbott (University of Adelaide) and genealogist Colleen Fitzpatrick to be Carl "Charles" Webb (b. 1905).
- **Format:** YouTube Short, 9:16, murder/mystery band (55–80 s). Series: *Cold File*.
- **Voice:** Kokoro `am_onyx`, speed 1.06 (test render). **203 words → 72.7 s measured narration.** Target 72 s.
- **Machine file:** `script.json`. Storyboard: `STORYBOARDS/MURDER-02/STORYBOARD.md`. Fact-check: `QUALITY_CONTROL/MURDER-02/FACTCHECK.md`.
- **Key accuracy decision:** the Webb identification is presented as a *proposed* identity that police and the coroner **have not confirmed** (coronial investigation still open per ABC, July 2026; no finding located as of Nov 2026). Re-check before upload; if the coroner rules, update L18 + S18.
- **Respect:** the man is shown only as a tiny, faceless, distant silhouette; no remains, no exhumation imagery, no photo of Webb. The nurse whose number was in the book is **not named** (private individual, never charged). The phone number is blurred on screen.
- **Wording fix vs concept:** the concept said the scrap was "sewn into his pocket". Sources say it was found in a small fob pocket *sewn inside* the trouser pocket; the script says "hidden in a tiny pocket inside a dead man's trousers", and packaging avoids "sewn in".
