## Clean read (for TTS / SRT)

Thirteen people, trapped about four kilometres inside a flooded cave.
After nine days, a torch broke the surface.
The diver asked: how many of you?
A boy answered: thirteen. All of them alive.
Tham Luang cave, northern Thailand, 2018.
June 23rd: twelve young footballers and their coach went in.
Then heavy rain flooded the way out.
July 2nd: British divers John Volanthen and Rick Stanton found them.
Finding them was the easy part.
None of the boys had ever dived.
Former Thai Navy SEAL Saman Kunan was placing air tanks along the route.
On the way back, he ran out of air.
He did not survive.
The plan that followed sounded impossible.
Each boy was sedated and fitted with a full-face mask.
Expert divers guided them out, one by one.
July 8th: four out. July 9th: four more.
July 10th: the last four, and their coach.
Saman Kunan died helping make it possible.
Thirteen went in. Thirteen came out.

**Loop line:** Thirteen went in. Thirteen came out.
