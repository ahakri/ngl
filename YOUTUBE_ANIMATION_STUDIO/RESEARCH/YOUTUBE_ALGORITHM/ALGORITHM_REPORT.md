## 0. Method and limits (read first)
- Tags: **[OFFICIAL]** = stated by YouTube/Google (help center, blog.youtube, YouTube executives on the record); **[CREATOR RESEARCH]** = third-party data/analysis (vidIQ, Galloway, Metricool, etc.) or press reporting; **[HYPOTHESIS]** = our inference, to be tested on this channel.
- **Verification limit:** in this container `support.google.com` and `blog.youtube` could NOT be fetched directly (DNS blocked; youtube.com also unreachable). Every [OFFICIAL] claim below was verified through web-search results that quote/summarise the cited official page, and where possible cross-checked with independent press coverage (TechCrunch, Search Engine Journal, Tubefilter). Treat exact wording as "as reported in search snippet". To re-verify verbatim, allow `support.google.com` and `blog.youtube` in the environment network settings.

## 1. How recommendations work (what is documented)
| # | Claim | Tag / source |
|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | Stated goal of search & discovery: help viewers find videos they are most likely to watch and **maximise long-term viewer satisfaction**. | [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16533387 ; https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16089387 |
| 1.2 | Homepage/browse recommendations rely primarily on a viewer's **watch history**; search history also influences future recommendations. | [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16089387 |
| 1.3 | **Satisfaction surveys**: users are asked to rate videos they watched so the system understands satisfaction, "not just watch time". The 2021 blog post describes 1–5 star surveys measuring "valued watchtime"; also learns from clicks, watch time, shares, likes, dislikes ("over 80 billion pieces of information"). | [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16089387 ; https://blog.youtube/inside-youtube/on-youtubes-recommendation-system/ |
| 1.4 | **Relative vs absolute watch time**: discovery uses both; relative watch time (% viewed) matters more for short videos, absolute watch time more for long videos. | [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/141805 |
| 1.5 | **Shorts feed is a different system** from long-form browse: long-form recommendations start with a viewer choosing to tap a thumbnail; in the Shorts feed the video just plays. Shorts lead Todd Sherman: "the audience is the algorithm". A Short may get a few hundred/thousand views, then stall while the system searches for its audience. (Aug 2023 Creator Insider interview, reported.) | [OFFICIAL, reported by press] https://techcrunch.com/2023/08/25/youtube-demystifies-the-shorts-algorithm-views-and-answers-other-creator-questions/ ; https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-explains-how-shorts-algorithm-works/494953/ |
| 1.6 | Posting more Shorts does not by itself expand/reduce reach; lots of low-quality clips can hurt. Re-uploading to "re-trigger" the algorithm is "not a great strategy". Hashtags not required. | [OFFICIAL, reported] same as 1.5; https://benzinga.com/z/34020532 ; https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-shares-advice-for-shorts-creators-including-notes-on-hashtags-the/691835/ |
| 1.7 | Goodrow (VP Eng.): after responsibility, **satisfaction** is the top priority; "no explicit formula of view to sub percentage". | [OFFICIAL, reported] https://tubefilter.com/2021/09/15/youtube-creator-insider-recommendation-algorithm-faqs/ |
| 1.8 | Surfaces: browse (home), suggested (watch-next), search, Shorts feed, channel page, notifications, external. Shorts also appear in search results and on the channel Shorts tab, where a thumbnail/title is seen before playing. Google web search also shows Shorts in video carousels. | [OFFICIAL] for surfaces generally (Studio traffic-source reports); Google carousel claim = [CREATOR RESEARCH] https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-shorts-titles/ |
| 1.9 | We do NOT know weights, batch sizes, "seed audience" sizes or any pass/fail threshold. Vendor figures (e.g. "70% viewed rate to advance", "batches of 200–500 viewers") cite no YouTube source; fliki explicitly notes they are unsupported. | [CREATOR RESEARCH] https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-shorts-algorithm/ ; https://fliki.ai/blog/youtube-shorts-algorithm |

**[HYPOTHESIS] model for planning:** a new Short is shown in-feed to small samples of viewers whose history matches it; if those viewers (a) don't swipe, (b) watch a high % / rewatch, (c) engage, and (d) don't give negative feedback, it is shown to more similar viewers. Consistent with 1.4–1.6 but the mechanics are not documented.

## 4. Metrics: what they mean for Shorts
- **CTR** [OFFICIAL concept https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314486]: impression counts when thumbnail ≥50% visible ≥1 s. For Shorts in-feed there is no tap decision, so CTR mainly reflects search/channel-page/shelf traffic. [HYPOTHESIS] For feed Shorts, **viewed-vs-swiped is the functional equivalent of CTR**.
- **Retention**: relative watch time weighted more for short videos [OFFICIAL 1.4]. Benchmarks conflict widely [CREATOR RESEARCH]: Quso median ~45% view-through, top 10% ~91% (https://quso.ai/research/video-retention-benchmarks); vidIQ reports Shorts average 73.6% APV (https://vidiq.com/blog/post/increase-audience-retention-youtube/); Metricool 2026 (799,718 videos): average Shorts view duration fell ~67% YoY to ~16 s while views rose >127% — partly an artifact of first-frame view counting (https://metricool.com/youtube-trends/). → Compare only against our own channel.
- **Session continuation / satisfaction**: officially YouTube optimises long-term satisfaction (1.1, 1.3). Whether "viewer kept watching YouTube after your video" is a direct signal is not documented for Shorts. [HYPOTHESIS] content that ends clean (no bait, payoff delivered) helps satisfaction surveys and reduces "Don't recommend channel".
- **Returning viewers**: tracked as casual/regular; officially no effect on reach (Sec. 2). [HYPOTHESIS] still the best proxy for whether a *series* works.

## 6. Hooks, loops, captions, titles, cadence
- **Hook window**: the in-feed decision is near-instant; vendors cite 1–3 s [CREATOR RESEARCH https://vidiq.com/blog/post/viral-video-hooks-youtube-shorts/]. The only official measurement is viewed-vs-swiped. [HYPOTHESIS] first frame must already be "mid-story" (no logo, no fade-in, no title card).
- **Loops**: no official statement that loops are rewarded. Replays do count as public views since Mar 2025 [OFFICIAL, Sec. 2] and add watch time (>100% retention) [CREATOR RESEARCH]. Whether loop-replays count as *engaged* views is not documented. [HYPOTHESIS] loops help APV; don't sacrifice a satisfying payoff for a fake loop.
- **Captions**: no Shorts-specific study found. Muted-viewing stats (e.g. "85% of Facebook video views silent", "92% of mobile users watch muted") are old, cross-platform, vendor-cited; Bytecap notes Shorts viewers are historically *more* sound-on than Reels viewers. [CREATOR RESEARCH] https://www.3playmedia.com/blog/studies-find-captions-improve-engagement ; https://www.bytecap.io/blog/science-of-silent-viewing-short-form-video-retention . [HYPOTHESIS] burned-in captions still help comprehension of TTS narration and proper nouns (cave names, case names); also upload an SRT for accessibility/search.
- **Titles/hashtags**: hashtags optional [OFFICIAL, reported 1.6]; vidIQ reports Shorts titles now behave more like search titles [CREATOR RESEARCH https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-shorts-titles/]. Title is visible in feed overlay, search, channel page, Google carousels. #shorts tag has no verified effect.
- **Cadence**: no official number; YouTube says volume itself doesn't change reach, low-quality volume can hurt [OFFICIAL, reported]. Third-party advice ranges 2–7/week (unsourced). vidIQ 10M-channel study (long-form) shows correlation, not causation [CREATOR RESEARCH https://vidiq.com/blog/post/How-Often-Post-on-Youtube/].
- **Shorts ↔ long-form**: no verified official statement that Shorts never hurt long-form. AIR (18,000 channels) found mixed channels have more subs, but Shorts >~55% of output correlates with weaker long-form; academic paper (arXiv 2402.18208) found long-form views/engagement declined on channels after adopting Shorts. Evidence is mixed. [CREATOR RESEARCH] https://air.io/en/audience-growth/do-youtube-shorts-help-your-long-form-videos-grow-data-from-18000-channels ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.18208
