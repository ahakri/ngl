## Thumbnail / cover concepts (3D render + ≤3 words)
### T1 — "The long way up" (primary)
- **Composition:** Vertical cross-section of the sinkhole as a 3D cutaway. A tiny bright pool at the very top, then a vast black chamber. At the bottom (lowest 25% of frame) a single diver silhouette with one torch beam angled down at an abstract dark shape in the silt. A thin white line runs all the way up to the pin-prick of daylight.
- **Focal subject:** the lone torch at the bottom.
- **Colour contrast:** near-black frame, cyan rock edges, one warm-white beam, red text.
- **Text:** `270 M` (red, upper-left third).
- **One visual idea:** how impossibly far the surface is.
- **Curiosity gap:** why is someone alone that deep, and what is the torch pointing at?

### T2 — "Two lights"
- **Composition:** Close 3D shot in black water. Two diver silhouettes, one above the other, connected by a single white line loop. The upper one holds the torch and the lower one is dark and still (abstract, no detail). Silt hangs around both.
- **Focal subject:** the line loop between them.
- **Colour contrast:** black/teal with a single cyan rim light; white line pops.
- **Text:** `WENT BACK` (white with dark stroke, upper third).
- **One visual idea:** one diver tied to another.
- **Curiosity gap:** who is he going back for, and why is the line wrapped around both?
