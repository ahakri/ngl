## Shorts description (≤ 3 lines visible)
Hagley Wood, Worcestershire, 18 April 1943: four boys found a skull inside a hollow wych elm. Police recovered the skeleton of a woman, dead about 18 months; she has never been identified. In 1944 messages began appearing on walls ("Who put Bella down the Wych Elm?"), and the writer was never found. A witchcraft theory was dismissed and a spy-ring story never proven. Her skeleton and key records are now missing.
