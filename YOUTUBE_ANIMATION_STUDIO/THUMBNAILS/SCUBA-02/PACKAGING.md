## Thumbnail / chosen-frame concepts
### A — "Two Dots" (primary)
- **Composition:** top-down aerial of a vast teal sea; two tiny heads in the lower third; a thin white boat wake curving off the top edge, boat itself almost out of frame.
- **Focal subject:** the two heads (small, so the emptiness is the point).
- **Colour contrast:** saturated teal sea vs two dark heads with small yellow mask straps; white wake line leads the eye from heads to the departing boat.
- **Text:** "LEFT BEHIND" (white, Anton, top-centre).
- **One visual idea:** the boat is leaving and they are not on it.
- **Curiosity gap:** how does a boat forget two people — and what happened next?
### B — "The Count"
- **Composition:** close-up of a clipboard with tally marks, one pair of marks circled in red; behind it, out of focus, an empty sea.
- **Focal subject:** the red-circled pair of tally marks.
- **Colour contrast:** white paper + red circle vs blurred blue background.
- **Text:** "COUNTED TWICE?" (question mark keeps it honest — the double-count is one account).
- **One visual idea:** a head count that was wrong.
- **Curiosity gap:** who was counted twice, and who wasn't counted at all?

## Shorts description
In January 1998, Americans Tom and Eileen Lonergan were left behind on a dive trip at St Crispin's Reef, Great Barrier Reef. They were never found; their story later inspired the film Open Water (2003). Animated reconstruction; no likenesses used. Some details (the head count, the dive slate) are disputed and labelled as such. Sources in the pinned comment.
