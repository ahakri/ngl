## Thumbnail / cover concepts (3D render + ≤3 words)
### T1 — "Thirteen on the ledge" (primary)
- **Composition:** Inside the chamber looking up a steep mud slope. In the upper half, 13 small faceless silhouettes huddle on a ledge. In the lower third, black water, with a single diver's helmet torch breaking the surface and its beam slanting up across the figures.
- **Focal subject:** the beam hitting the group.
- **Colour contrast:** near-black with a brown mud tone; one warm-white beam; cyan rim on the figures; yellow text.
- **Text:** `13 ALIVE` (yellow, upper-left third).
- **One visual idea:** a light finding people who should not be alive.
- **Curiosity gap:** how did 13 people survive down there, and how do you get them out through that water?

### T2 — "The way out"
- **Composition:** 2.5D cross-section of the mountain. Tiny daylight entrance bottom-left, a long cyan route snaking up through blue flooded sections to a single bright dot (the ledge) top-right. One tiny diver silhouette is midway along the route.
- **Focal subject:** the bright dot at the end of the route.
- **Colour contrast:** black/deep-blue section, cyan line, warm dot, white text.
- **Text:** `4 KM` (white with dark stroke, upper third).
- **One visual idea:** how far underwater they are.
- **Curiosity gap:** how do you bring non-swimmers through 4 km of flooded cave?

## Shorts description (≤ 3 lines visible)
June 2018: twelve young footballers and their coach were trapped deep inside Tham Luang cave, Thailand, by flood water. Nine days later two British divers found them alive. Every one of them came out.
Animated retelling. Sources: AP, AFP, NPR, SCMP, NBC, Time, CNBC (NEJM letter), CBS. Full list pinned in comments.
In memory of Saman Kunan, former Thai Navy SEAL, who died during the rescue.
