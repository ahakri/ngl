## Shorts description
6 Feb 2014: five Finnish cave divers cut a hole in the ice at Plura, northern Norway, to swim through the mountain to another cave. Two did not come back. Part 2: the friends who went back for them.
Animated retelling; contested details are labelled on screen. Sources: BBC, Helsingin Sanomat, The Local, AP, MTV Uutiset. Full list pinned in comments.
In memory of the two divers who died in Plura.
