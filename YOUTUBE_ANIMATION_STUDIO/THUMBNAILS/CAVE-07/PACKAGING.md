## Shorts description (≤ 3 lines visible)
6 April 1994, Zacatón, Mexico: cave-diving pioneer Sheck Exley (45), the first person to log 1,000 cave dives, and Jim Bowden made simultaneous attempts toward 1,000 ft on separate lines. Bowden turned back at about 925 ft with a gas problem and set a world record. Exley did not return. His body came up with the guideline three days later; the cause of death was never determined.
Animated reconstruction. Sources: Wikipedia, Sports Illustrated (1994), Undercurrent, aquaCorps/TDI-SDI, NSS Cave Diving Section, UT Austin (DEPTHX). Full list pinned.
