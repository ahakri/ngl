## Shorts description (≤ 3 lines visible)
Winchester Mystery House, San Jose, California: a staircase that climbs into the ceiling and a door that opens onto thin air. Legend says rifle heiress Sarah Winchester built such things to confuse the ghosts of Winchester rifle victims, on a medium's warning. The record: she kept adding to an 1880s farmhouse until her death in 1922; the 1906 earthquake cut the house from seven floors to four; the stairs are most likely remodeling leftovers; and her biographer Mary Jo Ignoffo found no evidence for the ghost story, which became the attraction's sales pitch after 1923.
Legend labelled as folklore; dramatised. Sources: Ignoffo (Captive of the Labyrinth), KQED, 99% Invisible, Patch, Smithsonian, Atlas Obscura, HowStuffWorks, HISTORY, Wikipedia. Full list pinned.
