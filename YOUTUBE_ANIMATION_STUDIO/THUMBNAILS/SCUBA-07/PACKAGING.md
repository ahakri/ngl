## Shorts description (≤ 3 lines visible)
In 1900, Greek sponge divers sheltering from a storm off Antikythera found a 1st-century-BC wreck about 50 m down. The salvage cost one diver's life. In 1902 a corroded lump from it split open and showed a gear wheel; CT scans in 2005 revealed an astronomical calculator that predicted eclipses.
Animated reconstruction. Sources: Nature (Freeth et al. 2006), WHOI, Cambridge, Scientific American, History.com, Alexander Jones (NYU). Full list pinned.
