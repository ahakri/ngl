## Shorts description (≤ 3 lines visible)
Blue Bell Hill, on the A229 in Kent: since at least 1974, drivers have reported hitting a woman or girl who then vanished. In 1974 a driver said he covered her with a blanket and fetched police; only the blanket was there. Motorists reported the same in 1992. Nothing has ever been confirmed, and folklorists note the "vanishing road ghost" is told worldwide.
Eyewitness claims, dramatised. Sources: ASSAP, KentOnline, Kent Archaeological Society, Brian Haughton, Snopes, Scientific American. Full list pinned.
