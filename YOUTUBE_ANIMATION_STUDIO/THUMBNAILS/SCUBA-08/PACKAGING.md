## Shorts description (≤ 3 lines visible)
Shackleton's Endurance was trapped and crushed by Weddell Sea pack ice in 1915; all 28 men survived. On 5 March 2022 the Endurance22 expedition's underwater drone found the wreck 3,008 m down, about four miles from Captain Frank Worsley's recorded position: upright, intact, name still on the stern. It's a protected Antarctic Treaty monument, and nothing may be removed. It was found 100 years to the day after Shackleton's funeral.
Animated reconstruction, no real footage. Sources: Falklands Maritime Heritage Trust / Endurance22, NPR, Live Science, Scientific American, National Geographic, Smithsonian, Saab, UK Antarctic Heritage Trust. Full list pinned.
