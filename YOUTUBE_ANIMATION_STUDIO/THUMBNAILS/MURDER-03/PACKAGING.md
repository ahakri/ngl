## Shorts description (paste-ready)

On 24 November 1971, a man ticketed as "Dan Cooper" hijacked Northwest Orient Flight 305, swapped the passengers for $200,000 and four parachutes in Seattle, then jumped from the 727's rear stairs over southwest Washington in a storm. In 1980, a boy smoothing sand on the Columbia River found about $5,800 of the ransom, with serial numbers that matched. No one has ever been charged. Where do you think he landed?

Sources: FBI (Famous Cases: D.B. Cooper; Seattle field office update, July 2016) · AP via CBS News, July 2016 · NPR, July 2016 · HistoryLink · National Archives at Seattle. Animated dramatisation; no real footage. Names and faces are not depicted.
