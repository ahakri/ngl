Ötzi (Otzi) the Iceman was found by two hikers in the Ötztal Alps on 19 September 1991. He lived around 3300 BC. In 2001 an X-ray revealed a flint arrowhead in his left shoulder; it was visible, in hindsight, on a 1991 film. 2007 CT scans showed it tore an artery. Who shot him is unknown.
