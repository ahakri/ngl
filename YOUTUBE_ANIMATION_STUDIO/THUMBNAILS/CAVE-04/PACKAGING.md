## Thumbnail / cover concepts (3D render + ≤3 words)
### T1 — "The funnel" (primary)
- **Composition:** 2.5D side cross-section of pale rock. A crawlway along the top, and a funnel-shaped crack plunging diagonally down to a pinch point. At the pinch, a small grey outline figure, inverted (no detail). A single taut rope runs from a ceiling anchor down to the figure's feet.
- **Focal subject:** the inverted outline at the narrowest point.
- **Colour contrast:** beige/grey rock on black, cyan rock edges, one red anchor point, white text.
- **Text:** `27 HOURS` (white, upper third). Fallback if re-verification changes the figure: `NO WAY OUT`.
- **One visual idea:** a body-shaped space too small to turn around in.
- **Curiosity gap:** how do you get someone out of that, and why didn't it work?

### T2 — "The anchor"
- **Composition:** Extreme close-up 3D render of a bolt anchor in a cave ceiling, with a crack running through the rock around it. A pulley and a taut rope lead down out of frame into darkness. Dust hangs in a single warm helmet-lamp beam.
- **Focal subject:** the crack around the bolt.
- **Colour contrast:** warm lamp orange vs black; crack edges in red; white text.
- **Text:** `IT HELD…` (2 words, upper third).
- **One visual idea:** the one piece of metal everything depended on.
- **Curiosity gap:** "it held… until?" The viewer knows it fails and wants the story.
