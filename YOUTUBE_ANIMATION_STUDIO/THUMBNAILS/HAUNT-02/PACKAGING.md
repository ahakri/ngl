## Thumbnail / cover-frame concepts

**Concept 1: "ROOM 217" (recommended)**
- Composition: 9:16. A long hotel corridor in deep shadow on a strong one-point perspective. Door 217 on the right, ajar, with a blade of warm light across the red carpet. At the far end, barely visible, a faceless maid silhouette with an apron.
- Focal subject: the brass "217" lit by the spill.
- Colour contrast: cold blue-black corridor against a warm amber door-light; red carpet.
- Text: **ROOM 217** (2 words), upper third.
- The one visual idea: the door is open and someone is at the end of the hall.
- Curiosity gap: who is in the room, and who is the figure?

**Concept 2: "SHE SURVIVED"**
- Composition: Room 217 interior from the doorway. An open, empty suitcase on the rack; clothes folded in neat squares on the white bed; the window shows snowy mountains in fog.
- Focal subject: the neatly folded clothes (the wrong detail).
- Colour contrast: warm bedside lamp against a cold blue window; a white bedspread as the brightest area.
- Text: **SHE SURVIVED** (2 words); optional small "1911" stamp.
- The one visual idea: the room has been tidied by someone, while the text says the "ghost" never died.
- Curiosity gap: a direct contradiction (survivor vs ghost) makes the viewer ask how both can be true.

(No real hotel photos, no logos, no film stills, no faces.)
