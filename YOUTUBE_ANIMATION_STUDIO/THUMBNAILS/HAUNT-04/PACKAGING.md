## Thumbnail / cover-frame concepts (custom Shorts thumbnail for search, channel grid and related links)

**Concept 1: "PRINTED 1936" (recommended)**
- Composition: 9:16. Dark oak staircase from the foot, three-quarter angle; the translucent veiled figure (our design) mid-descent on the half-landing, upper-middle third; flash glare bloom in the lower-left corner.
- Focal subject: the pale figure against the dark panelling.
- Colour contrast: near-black brown oak, cold grey window light, one luminous pale shape; text white with dark stroke, pale-green accent underline.
- Text: **PRINTED 1936** (2 words).
- The one visual idea: a magazine published this.
- Curiosity gap: why would a respected magazine print a ghost, and was it faked?

**Concept 2: "TWO IMAGES?"**
- Composition: split vertical: top half the empty staircase; bottom half the same staircase with the figure; a thin pale-green seam where they overlap, like two plates sliding together.
- Focal subject: the seam / overlap.
- Colour contrast: warm oak vs cold blue-grey; pale-green seam.
- Text: **TWO IMAGES?** (2 words).
- The one visual idea: the double-exposure explanation.
- Curiosity gap: is the ghost just two photos on one plate? The Short gives both sides.

(Never use the real 1936 photograph, the real magazine mastheads, or any portrait of Dorothy Walpole.)
