## Shorts description
In September 2014, Egyptian diver Ahmed Gabr reached 332.35 m on scuba in the Red Sea off Dahab. The descent took about 12 minutes; the ascent, with decompression stops, took nearly 15 hours. In 2020 anonymous critics questioned the evidence. Guinness World Records reviewed it and still recognises the record. Animated reconstruction; no real footage or likeness used. Sources are in the pinned comment.
