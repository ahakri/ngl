## Thumbnail / cover-frame concepts (custom Shorts thumbnail for search, channel grid and related links)

**Concept 1: "BODY CHUTE" (recommended)**
- Composition: 9:16. Looking down the sloped concrete tunnel from the top. Bare bulbs recede into black; the empty steel gurney sits on the rails in the lower third, slightly off-centre, as if it has just started rolling.
- Focal subject: the empty gurney under the nearest bulb.
- Colour contrast: warm sodium-yellow bulbs against cold blue-black concrete; the white sheet is the brightest point.
- Text: **BODY CHUTE** (2 words), upper third, white with a dark stroke, the quote marks implied by the THEORY-amber underline.
- The one visual idea: a tunnel made for the dead, with nobody pushing.
- Curiosity gap: why would a hospital need this, and why is the gurney moving?

**Concept 2: "63,000?"**
- Composition: split vertical. The top 55% is the five-storey facade at dusk with 150 dark window openings and one faint lit bay on the fourth floor. The bottom 45% is black with "63,000" struck through in red.
- Focal subject: the struck-through number.
- Colour contrast: blue-grey dusk, one warm window, red strike line, white numerals.
- Text: **63,000?** (1 word/number).
- The one visual idea: the famous number is wrong.
- Curiosity gap: then how many, and what still walks the fourth floor? Signals honesty (myth-bust) while keeping the haunt open.
