## Thumbnail / cover-frame concepts (custom Shorts thumbnail for search, channel grid and related links)

**Concept 1: "NOBODY WAS THERE" (recommended)**
- Composition: 9:16, infrared monochrome. Overgrown clearing, tilted slab headstones, bare trees glowing white against a black sky. The checkerboard-faced stone sits dead centre in the lower half, EMPTY, with a flattened patch of grass in front of it.
- Focal subject: the empty checkered stone.
- Colour contrast: pure IR black-and-white; the only colour is the pale-green caption accent.
- Text: **NOBODY WAS THERE** (3 words), upper third.
- The one visual idea: an empty seat that should not be empty.
- Curiosity gap: the viewer knows something showed up on the film and has to watch to find out what. It also avoids showing any ghost figure on the cover.

**Concept 2: "DOUBLE EXPOSURE?"**
- Composition: the same IR stone, with our recreated faceless seated figure at ~30% opacity, offset by a few pixels like a misregistered second frame; a thin film-rebate border with sprocket holes on the left edge.
- Focal subject: the ghosted, doubled figure.
- Colour contrast: monochrome IR plus a single orange DISPUTED tag in the corner.
- Text: **DOUBLE EXPOSURE?** (2 words).
- The one visual idea: the sceptic's explanation made visible.
- Curiosity gap: is it a trick of the film or not? It names the debate honestly and pulls in sceptics as well as believers.

(Never use or trace the real 1991 photo; the figure is our generic recreation and is labelled as such in the video.)
