## Thumbnail / cover-frame concepts (custom Shorts thumbnail for search, channel grid and related links)

**Concept 1: "DON'T STOP" (recommended)**
- Composition: 9:16 POV down the foggy road, headlight cones converging at centre; the pale faceless figure on the right verge at the third line, small but sharp.
- Focal subject: the pale figure; everything else desaturated blue-black.
- Colour contrast: cold blue night, warm headlight cones, one pale near-white figure; text white with dark stroke, pale-green underline accent.
- Text: **DON'T STOP** (2 words), upper third.
- The one visual idea: a stranger waiting for you on a dark road.
- Curiosity gap: why not? Who is she? The answer (a legend vs a cemetery's truck) is only in the Short.

**Concept 2: "BENT BARS"**
- Composition: tight on the generic cemetery gate bars at night, two bars bowed outward, torch beam from lower left, faint hand-shaped marks at hand height; behind the bars, a faint pale shape in fog.
- Focal subject: the gap between the two bent bars.
- Colour contrast: black iron, white torch light, a red/blue police wash on one edge.
- Text: **1976** (1 word/number).
- The one visual idea: something wanted out (or in).
- Curiosity gap: what bent them? (The Short gives the cemetery's truck explanation honestly.)
