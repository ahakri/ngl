## Removed / not used
- Names of the 2016 victims and the third diver, plus the 2013 father and son and the 2018 victim. Not named, per brief.
- "Size of a cathedral" (concept). No source, so it was replaced with "huge room".
- Cause-of-death speculation (narcosis, gas, collapse). The concept beat "why it's deadly: depth, narcosis, gas" is not narrated as the cause of any death. Only the documented depth is given.
- Exact Ballroom depth (127/130 ft) and chimney depth. Inconsistent figures, so they are not narrated.
- The 2013 Christmas deaths and the 2018 death. Not narrated (length), and noted here only to justify "at least".
- No family quotes (e.g. the father who argued the cave should stay open) are used.
