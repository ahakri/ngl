## Removed / softened during scripting

- **"Minus thirty"** (concept hook): only one popular source gives −30 °C (−22 °F); no second independent figure found → **removed from narration**.
- **"Footprints in socks… walked into minus thirty"** → now "walked into the snow. In socks. Some barefoot."
- **"Yetis"** (concept hook) → dropped (adds nothing, invites mockery of the dead).
- **"Science finally solved Dyatlov Pass"** (concept alt title) → **not used**; the authors explicitly don't claim a solution. Packaging uses question/"may explain" framing.
- **Slab "the size of a car"** (Mental Floss phrasing) → not narrated (single popular source; paper figures not checked directly).
- **"Theory cards stamped RULED OUT"** (concept) → changed to "NO EVIDENCE"/"UNPROVEN" to stay defensible.
- **Prosecutors' date:** concept said "2020 prosecutors' review"; reinvestigation began 2019, conclusion announced July 2020 — narration says "In 2020 … blamed", which is accurate.
- No victim names in narration (respect + pace); end card reads "In memory of the nine, 1959".

## Residual risk

- Distances and some forensic details vary between accounts; all such numbers in narration carry "about" or are shown only on screen.
- The study is contested (e.g. J. McElwaine questioned slab stiffness/speed); narration frames it as a model ("their model", "if they're right").
