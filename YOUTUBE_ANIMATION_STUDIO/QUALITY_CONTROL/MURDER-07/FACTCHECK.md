## Removed / not used
- **"Police thought he died last winter"** (concept hook). Not sourced in that form; replaced with "everyone assumed it was a climber who'd died recently".
- **Bled to death "within minutes".** Time to death is debated; not narrated.
- **Head injury / blow to the head; fight theories (blood of others on his kit), being chased.** Debated or theory; not narrated, no suspect invented.
- **Damage to the body during the 1991 recovery** (jackhammer, ski pole). True but unnecessary and borderline disrespectful; not used.
- **Exact arrow counts, the 2001 radiologist's name.** Sources differ / not needed.
- **Museum cell temperature.** The snippet figure was inconsistent; not used.
- **The finders' names.** Public, but not needed in narration (named in FACTCHECK/description only).

## Visual ethics check
- No gore, no face, no hands. Ötzi appears only as an unreadable dark form under ice / frost, a covered form in the CT room, or a distant silhouette (S14, labelled DRAMATISATION).
- X-ray and CT images are original stylised line art, not traced from the real radiographs.
- Memorial card: "ÖTZI  c. 3300 BC".
