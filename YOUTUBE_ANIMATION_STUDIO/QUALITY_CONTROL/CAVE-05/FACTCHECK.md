## Removed / not used
- "He went back to the cave a month later" (concept). Not confirmed in this session's snippets, so it was dropped.
- "37 m of rock" (drill depth). Single source, so it was dropped.
- Age (54 vs 55). Disputed, so it is not narrated.
- Rescuer names (e.g. the surname "Clamour" in one snippet). Single source, and not needed.
- Quotes: "I was buried alive", "bowl of cocoa", and his reasoning about who should go ("skinnier, needed less air"). No quotes are used. The reasoning is ambiguous in snippets, so it is not narrated.
- The concept hook "His air was gone. So he came up inside the rock." Reworded to the documented gas-sharing framing.
- The concept beat "Partner thinks he's dead" was inverted to the documented version: Gràcia believed his partner hadn't made it.

## Visual ethics check
- Both men survived. Figures are faceless divers in masks, and no real photos are used.
- Hallucination shot: abstract glows only, labelled DRAMATISATION.
- Emergency lights at the surface are generic, with no Guardia Civil markings.
- The light-ration flashes in S09 are capped at ≤2 Hz (photosensitivity).
