# CAVE-03 Animation Notes (Plura, Norway 2014)

Builders: `BLENDER_PROJECTS/CAVE-03/shots.py` + shared `PIPELINE/blender/lib_cave.py`.
Preview rounds: 1 full, then 2 targeted (10 shots, then 5 shots).
Fin colours: A = blue (stuck), B = white (got through), C = orange (turned back), other team-two divers = green and magenta.

| Shot | Built | Deviations / weaknesses |
|---|---|---|
| S01 | Under-ice view: ice slab with boolean triangle hole, plug still attached, glowing kerfs, saw bar finishing the third side, plug lifted out (light floods in), chips, bubbles, glove drops a weighted white line | None |
| S02 | 2.5D mountain section: snow outline, PLURA pond entrance, U-shaped route band, pulsing red squeeze ring, STEINUGLEFLÅGET exit. Cyan line draws while the camera pulls out from the entrance | The map overlay became a stamp plus a depth gauge (the 3D diagram already does the map's job). |
| S03 | Snowy valley: low-poly snowy mountains, bare trees, frozen pond with dark triangle, 5 drysuit silhouettes with twin tanks, sled with tanks, snowfall, breath vapour, descending crane | The mountains are faceted. |
| S04 | Same framing at dusk: 3 figures, footprints where the other two stood, work lamp, snow | None |
| S05 | 3D map: Scandinavia outline (approximate coastline), borders, dashed Arctic Circle, country labels, pin drops on PLURA, zoom in | The coastline is hand-approximated. The map overlay became a stamp. |
| S06 | U section: five cyan dots travel the route, arrow draws on, rope-ladder icon in the dry exit, pan left to right | The dots bunch together. |
| S07 | Marble tunnel, team one (blue and white fins) passes left to right on scooters, white flash mid-shot (time jump), then three team-two divers enter | Heavy blue fog; marble walls read as soft cloud. |
| S08 | Big tunnel ends at a rock face with a low flattened slot (boolean ellipse), restriction tunnel r=0.8 beyond, boulders, silt bed, two torch pools converging on the slot, push-in | The torch pools on the face show as two soft discs before they converge. |
| S09 | Over-the-shoulder from the far side: diver B emerges from the slot, then turns his whole body back; rack focus from 3 m to 5.7 m; buddy's glow behind the slot through silt | The fins come close to camera at the end. |
| S10 | POV through the slot: stuck buddy as abstract silhouette (tanks, head shape), torch blinking in a signal pattern, white cord looped from the slot edge around the valve, silt volume, rising shake | None |
| S11 | Silt settles (volume density falls), the light behind the slot stays still, diver B hangs in his beam, slow pull-back | Very dark; diver B is small. |
| S12 | Section: grey dot stopped at the squeeze, cyan dot beyond, three cyan team-two dots entering, squeeze pulsing red | The timer overlay became text "TEAM 2". |
| S13 | Deep wide tunnel, three team-two silhouettes with torches; one drifts off the line and stops with its beam still | **Weakest shot.** The silhouettes barely read; it shows mostly as beams in the dark. It could use a closer camera and a rim light. |
| S14 | Over the shoulder of diver C (orange fins, twins): the slot ahead is filled by an abstract dark mass plus a tank and a hose; slow push stops short | None |
| S15 | Section: orange dot reverses back to PLURA, dotted trail pops on, pan right to left | The map overlay became text. |
| S16 | Diver C hangs upright on the line under the now-dim hole, bubbles, slow drift; last 19 frames cut to a blue emergency light flashing on snow | The timer became text "HOURS OF DECO" so I didn't invent a number. |
| S17 | Defocused snowy pond with red and white barrier tape around the hole, snowfall; NOTICE card overlay | Document style `notice` doesn't exist, so it uses a `card` with a red accent. |
| S18 | Under-ice composition: a bright triangle, 7 recovery-diver lights drop through one after another, camera returns to the S01 framing and holds | None |

Labels were normalised to DOCUMENTED FACT. "REPORTED" was not an allowed value.
