## Removed / not used
- Victims' names. Jari Huotarinen is confirmed (BBC, HS). The second victim's full name did not appear in any snippet we found (HS shows only "Jari U."), so neither name is narrated, to keep the treatment even and avoid a misspelling. Add names on the end card only after a full-text check.
- Cause of the second death (concept: "one diver gets into trouble; a second also dies"). Left unstated. A forum claim of rebreather malfunction is unverified.
- "130 m" as the depth of the incident. Sources give 110 m (restriction) and ~130 m (deepest point / bodies). Narration uses "over 100 metres".
- "The only way out was deeper" (concept title). This is literally true only for the through-route, so it is kept as a title, not as a narrated claim. The narration says what happened: the survivor turned back.
- "Operation Ghost", the names of the British official-recovery divers, and the 6-month investigation. Unverified or not needed, so omitted.
- The 10-hour dive figure and the 2-hour gap: BBC-only. The gap is kept with a REPORTED label, and the 10 h figure is omitted.
- No dialogue or quotes from survivors.
