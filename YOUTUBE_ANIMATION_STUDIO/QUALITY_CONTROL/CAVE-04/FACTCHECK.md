## Removed / not used
- "Medical student", Thanksgiving, family details: confirmed (AP, Wikipedia) but left out on purpose to keep the focus on the rescue and to respect the family.
- Exact distance and depth, the 18x10-inch crevice size, the passage name "Bob's Push".
- Time of death, cause of death, "more than 100 / 137 rescuers" (the 137 figure comes from an unreliable page).
- The concept hook "He thought it was a tunnel…" (mind-reading).
- Memorial plaque: mentioned in one snippet only, so it is not shown.
- The injured rescuer (one snippet only).
- No quotes from family or rescuers.
