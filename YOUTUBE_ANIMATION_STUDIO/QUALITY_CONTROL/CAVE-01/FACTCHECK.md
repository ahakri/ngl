## Removed / not used
- Concept hook line "Then it pulled him down." Removed: it implies the body physically dragged Shaw, which no source states.
- "15 minutes down / 12 hours up" (concept). Replaced with "Ten minutes down / about ten hours of stops", which better matches the 2005 dive sources.
- "Promise" to the family (alt title). No source quotes a promise, so it is not narrated. The alt title was reworded in PACKAGING.
- Shaw's age (50 vs 51 in different snippets), "Qantas", Dreyer's age of 20, and birth years on the memorial card. Not confirmed across 2 sources, so all were omitted.
- Body "heavier than planned" (concept beat). Not confirmed, so removed.
- No dialogue or quotes from Shaw, Shirley or the Dreyer family are used.

## Visual ethics check
- No remains are shown. The lost diver is an abstract dark shape (tank outline + fins), and the two rising bodies are silhouettes against the light.
- No real footage, photos or faces.
