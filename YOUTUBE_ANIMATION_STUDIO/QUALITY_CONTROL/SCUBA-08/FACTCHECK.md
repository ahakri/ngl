## Removed / not used
- **"Found after 107 years"** (concept title). Replaced by "over a century" (see claim 6).
- **The ice clock** ("They had days left before the ice trapped them too", concept hook). No source gave a days-left figure; a PA headline says the leader "feared running out of time", but the snippet had no detail. Not used.
- **Search-box size** (7 x 14 vs 8 x 15 miles) and **time at sea** (five weeks / "less than a month" / 40 days). Sources conflict; not used.
- **Quotes** (Shackleton's "She's going, boys"; Bound's "finest wooden shipwreck"). No quotes are used.
- **Letter material** (brass vs gold leaf) and the **name/star order**. Not narrated; the star sits below the name in S01, as Bound describes it.
- **"HMS Endurance"** (one headline). Not used.
- **The 2024 3D scan, named marine species, the wreck's exact orientation.** Not needed; not used.
- **Shackleton's death (5 Jan 1922).** Not used; only the funeral date (from E3/E14) appears.

## Visual ethics check
- Shackleton is not depicted. The crew appear only as tiny faceless silhouettes on the ice (S04), as a dramatised wide shot.
- The drone never touches the wreck: lamps hold at a distance and pull away (S13), matching the expedition's no-contact statements.
- The icebreaker and robots are generic (no logos, flags or real livery). The stern lettering reproduces the ship's name and star only, as described by the sources.
