## Removed / not used
- **That Exley tied himself to the line on purpose** (to save others a dangerous body recovery). Speculative; the concept fact-check says do NOT use it. Not narrated or implied.
- **Exley's dive computer depth (906 ft; Wikipedia gives a 879–906 ft range).** Verified, but it is shallower than Bowden's 925 ft turn, which would confuse viewers without explanation. Not narrated, so we don't need "kept going deeper" either, and we never say he went deeper than Bowden.
- **Cause of death (HPNS, narcosis).** Only theories; not narrated as fact.
- **"Deepest sinkhole in the world".** Contested; not used.
- **Exley's earlier depth records (e.g. Nacimiento del Río Mante).** Not confirmed in our searches; not used.
- **"Rule of thirds" credit.** The concept's draft beat credited Exley with the rule; its origin is not established in our sources, so it was not narrated.
- **Bowden's decompression / later health.** Not narrated.
- **Sulphur in the water.** Not confirmed (sources say warm/thermal, slightly acidic); the yellow-green tint in the shaft is atmosphere only.

## Visual ethics check
- No body, no face, no real photo. The recovery is a line, a pulley, tanks and a diagram coil.
- Divers are faceless silhouettes; the present-day diver in S16–S17 is generic, not Exley.
- The book card is generic (no real cover art). The incident card is labelled as our summary, not an official document.
