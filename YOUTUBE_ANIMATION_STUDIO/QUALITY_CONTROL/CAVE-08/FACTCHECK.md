## Removed / not used
- **"Missing for 22 years"** (concept alt title). Corrected to 21 (J1; 1979 → 2000).
- **"People swim here every summer"** (concept hook). Swimming has been cancelled in drought years (J4, J12); replaced by "swimming hole".
- **"A tunnel that seals behind you" / the "false exit" chamber.** Not sourced (only one low-quality page repeats a "false exit" story); not used, as in the concept's own v2 correction.
- **The recovery diver half-buried to the waist in sliding gravel, pulled free as his air ran out.** J2 only (Bond; the Find a Grave copy is the same article). Not narrated, not shown (S11 shows the recovery divers turning back, never a burial), not named.
- **"They went in late at night without backup lights or a safety line."** One account (J3), and it reads as blame. Not used; no time of day is shown in S07.
- **Their ages (20, 21) and hometown (Pasadena).** Single accounts; not narrated.
- **The skull, the dive-club patch identification and the "keyhole" in the floor.** Graphic or single-source detail; S16 shows only a dark gap in the floor and two beams.
- **The grate being found dismantled six months later.** J2 only.
- **An exact death toll.** DISPUTED; no number asserted.
- **Mouth width (12 ft vs 13 ft)** and **ledge jumping** (one page says jumping is forbidden; rules vary): neither narrated nor depicted (swimmers float; nobody jumps).
