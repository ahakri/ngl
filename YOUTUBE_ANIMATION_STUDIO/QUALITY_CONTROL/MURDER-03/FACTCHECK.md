## Removed or softened during scripting

- **Concept hook "Nine years later"**: wrong. Nov 1971 to Feb 1980 is about 8 years 3 months. Narration says "In 1980" instead.
- **"An 8-year-old dug some of it out"** became "smoothed sand for a campfire". He was levelling sand for a campfire, which is the documented account.
- **Exact jump parameters** (altitude about 10,000 ft, about 200 mph, flaps 15 deg, gear down): the 7 searches found no second source, so they are **not used** anywhere.
- **"Dark suit, sunglasses" costume details**: no second source in this check. The visuals use a generic faceless silhouette and the narration does not describe clothing.
- **Exact passenger count (36)**: dropped from narration because counts vary. Kept on screen as the majority figure.
- **Ticket price ($18.52)**: not confirmed, so not used.
- **"Theory ladder" naming suspects** (concept beat): **removed**. No suspect is named. Named suspects were never charged and some relatives are living. The "wall of suspects" shot uses blank, nameless cards.
- **"FBI gave up"** (concept wording) became "stopped actively investigating". The FBI reassigned resources but did not formally declare the case closed forever.
- **Cooper vane date** ("November 1972" in one blog): not used. The vane is attributed to the FAA after the 1972 copycats.

## Ethics notes

- No one was physically harmed in the hijacking. No victims are depicted. The hijacker is a faceless silhouette, and no real photos, sketches or FBI composite are used.
- The boy who found the money is not named or depicted with any likeness.
- The FBI letterhead, seal and real note wording are **not** reproduced. Document overlays are marked "recreation" or "paraphrase".
