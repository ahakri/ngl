## Removed or kept out of the narration

- **REMOVED**: the claim that Bill Wilkins's son identified the voice (an LBC phone-in or the "BBC *Hauntings* 2024" story). It appears only on weak podcast/blog pages and a Wikipedia-derived snippet, and a direct search found no corroboration. It is not used.
- **REMOVED**: the voice's specific words (going blind, haemorrhage, "died in the chair in the corner downstairs"). These would be a direct quote of the recording; the precise wording is reported in popular sources only. We say only "claimed it died in that house".
- **NOT USED**: the levitation / "flying Janet" photographs (Graham Morris). They are disputed (the photographer's own theory is that she jumped), and they are real photos of a child.
- **NOT USED**: *The Conjuring 2*'s demonic framing and Ed and Lorraine Warren's role. Film invention or contested; only the fact that the film is based on the case is used.
- **NOT USED**: the claim that Grosse and Playfair pressed the girls to retract their confessions (E2, snippet; single chain of attribution). It is too strong an allegation against named people for this length.
- **NOT USED**: the claim that the SPR suppressed Gregory's report (single author, Couttie).
- **UNCERTAIN, worded around**: the exact date of the police call. Only "August 1977" is used, not a day.
- **UNCERTAIN, worded around**: the end of the activity (1978 vs 1979). The narration avoids an end date. The concept says 1977–79.
