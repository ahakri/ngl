## Changes after re-check (applied to script.json, SCRIPT.md and STORYBOARD.md)

1. **L10:** "A warehouse worker in Belgium." became "A programmer in Belgium." The S09 map caption now reads "programmer, author of AZdecrypt – Belgium".
2. **L14:** "The warehouse worker's software attacked each one." became "The Belgian's program attacked each one."
3. **L15:** "Then words surfaced. Trying to catch me." became "Then, real English surfaced." The S14 title changed from TRYING TO CATCH ME to REAL WORDS, and the decode shows a neutral plaintext fragment.
4. All other ⚠ items passed with 2–3 sources and no wording change: the diagonal "down one row, across two", "hundreds of thousands", the FBI acknowledgement, the TV-show reference, the 408 solved in about a week, November 1969 mailing, five victims, and Z13/Z32.

## Removed or softened (from scripting, still in force)

- "FBI confirmed" is written as "FBI acknowledged". This is conservative; [IT] and [NH] report the FBI also said it verified the claim.
- The exact figure of 650,000 is not narrated.
- The plaintext lines about the gas chamber, "slaves" and paradise are omitted.
- The 37-victims boast, all suspect names, and the 2021 private Z13 "solution" name are omitted.
- No crime-scene locations or victims' names are used. Cipher glyphs are labelled stand-ins.
