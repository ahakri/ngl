## Removed / not used
- **"About five minutes on the bottom"** (concept beat). Only one unidentified snippet; another search found no source. Not narrated.
- **Who first spotted the gear (Valerios vs Spyridon Stais) and the exact day.** Disputed (A6); nobody is named.
- **"The world's first computer"** as a narrated fact. A popular label (A16, UCL press release title), not a formal classification. Used only in packaging, in quotes, as a description.
- **"Decades ignored"** (concept). The gear was noticed within about a year (1902), so this framing was dropped; L09–L10 say it split in 1902 and the full picture took until the 2005 scans.
- **Exact gear count.** Sources differ (27 in Price's main fragment, "at least 30" elsewhere); not narrated. The reconstruction shows a generic gear train.
- **The diver's sighting of "corpses" / horses** (an Elias Stadiatis anecdote in popular accounts). Single-source colour; not used.
- **Olympic Games dial, planets, Hipparchus/Archimedes attribution.** True or debated, but not needed; not used.
- **"First underwater archaeological excavation".** Cambridge says "one of the first"; not narrated.

## Visual ethics check
- Divers are faceless (dark faceplates). The death is shown only as an empty helmet and a seated, consoled diver; no injury.
- The reconstruction is generic, not a copy of a specific published model or museum photo.
