## Removed or kept out of the narration

- **REMOVED**: "printed in the Chicago Sun-Times" (and the *National Examiner*). Only one source chain (the archived About.com piece) states it and the original page was not seen. May go in the description as "reportedly" only.
- **REMOVED**: the photographer's name (spellings vary; brief instruction).
- **NOT USED**: claims that the photo "passed every test" or that an expert ruled out double exposure (promotional / low-quality sources).
- **NOT USED**: the pond as a 1920s mob body-dump, the drowned farmer, and the 1973 police sighting of a figure crawling from the pond (legend-only sources; crime/death content).
- **NOT USED**: the cemetery's founding date, last burial date (sources conflict) and individual burials (private families).
- **NOT USED**: the identity legends ("Mrs Rogers", the woman with a baby): only "who she was" is asked.

## Visual-ethics checks

- The real photo is not shown, traced or composited. The recreated figure is generic (faceless, seated, long dress), has a different pose detail (head turned away) and carries a DRAMATISATION badge "recreation, not the 1991 photo". The darkroom print is never readable.
- No real people's faces or names; GRS members are faceless silhouettes; no grave names on any headstone.
- The cemetery is a generic procedural clearing, not a map of the real plots.
