# FACTCHECK — SCUBA-05 "He Dived 332 m. Getting Back Took Nearly 15 Hours" (Ahmed Gabr, Dahab, 2014)

Checked 2026-11-27. Method: WebSearch (6 queries; claims confirmed from search-result excerpts of the pages listed). No page was fetched in full. Every narrated line is listed. Status key: **VERIFIED** (≥2 independent sources), **LABELLED** (narrated with a qualifier or attribution), **REMOVED** (cut from narration).

**Overall status: VERIFIED.** The 2020 dispute was researched. Guinness World Records reviewed anonymous claims made in summer 2020, concluded that Gabr did reach 332.35 m, and continues to recognise him as the record holder. The record was **not revoked**. No search returned a later revocation. The narration presents the dispute and the outcome, attributes the claims to anonymous critics, and states the GWR conclusion.

- Concept title figure "14 hours" was replaced by "nearly 15 hours" (A, D). The minority figure is noted above.
- Exact tank counts (60+ / ~70 of ~90) conflict, so the narration says "dozens".
- "Trained four years" and "17 years as an instructor" (GWR, single snippet) are not narrated.
- "Last 10 hours spent at 30 m or shallower" has a single source and is not narrated.
- Pressure multiple (~34 atm), narcosis, HPNS and gas-mix details were not searched, so they are not narrated.
- Pascal Bernabé's unratified 330 m claim is not narrated.
- Gabr's counter-claims about the critic's identity and motive are not narrated (see L14).
- Exact date of GWR's review conclusion: not returned by search, so the narration says only that "Guinness reviewed" and does not give a year for the verdict.
