## Source-quality note
The case's pre-crime details (footprints, attic footsteps, newspaper, key) are known from **neighbours' statements given after the crime**, not from anything police observed; secondary sources repeat them with variations. The script therefore labels them REPORTED and says "reportedly told neighbours" (L03). Popular blogs that claim an intruder "lived in the attic for weeks" were **not used**: the police record does not establish that.
