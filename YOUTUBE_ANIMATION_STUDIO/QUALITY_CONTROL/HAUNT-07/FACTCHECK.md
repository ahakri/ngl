## Removed / not used
- **The 1965 crash, the "ghost bride / bridesmaid" origin story, and any name or detail of the women who died.** Removed under the hard rule. The legend's origin is folklore, and naming or implying the victims would attach a ghost story to real dead people.
- **Witness names** (the 1974 bricklayer; the 1992 driver named in the concept). Not needed; not used.
- **Hitch-hiker variant** (a woman who flags down cars and vanishes from the back seat; dates conflict: 1962/1972, 1971/1972). Cut for length; covered only by the sceptics' "vanishing hitchhiker" card.
- **"No blood, no dent"** (concept hook) and **"He ran into the police station shaking"**. Not sourced; not used.
- **"Officially the most haunted place in the UK"** (Time Out headline from a survey/ranking). Not narrated.
- **The child's apparent age in the 1974 account.** Single account; not used.

## Visual ethics check
- The figure is generic, faceless, in a plain coat; seen for under half a second in S01 and small in the mirror in S12, both labelled DRAMATISATION.
- The impact is implied (flash + sound), never shown; no injury, no body. The blanket shot shows only its edge and the driver's hands.
- No real number plates, road signs or police insignia.
