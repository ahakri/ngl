# HAUNT-05 — Fact-check: The Tunnel Built to Hide the Dead

Checked 2026-11-27. Method: WebSearch (6 searches), building on the concepts fact-check v2 pass (dates confirmed; death toll set to ~6,000–8,000 against the 63,000 myth; "body chute" downgraded to *reported*). As with HAUNT-01, verification rests on **search-result snippets**; no page was opened in full. Before publishing, a human should open W1, W2 and W4 and confirm the items marked (snippet).

**Status: VERIFIED** for every narrated claim, with two caveats that are already built into the wording and the badges:
1. The tunnel's *use for moving bodies* is told consistently across Wikipedia, Atlas Obscura, Mental Floss and Grunge, but no primary record (hospital report, NRHP form) was reached. Narration says "reportedly" / "it's said" and the badge is **THEORY**.
2. The 6,000–8,000 figure is a historians' *estimate* reported by secondary sources (Grunge citing LiveScience; local-historian estimates). City records were reportedly lost in the 1937 flood, so no official total exists. Narration says "Historians estimate".

Pending (not needed for the narration, recommended before upload): open the NRHP nomination (npgallery.nps.gov asset 391d9b96…) or the Filson Historical Society resource list for a primary description of the tunnel; confirm the LiveScience article that Grunge cites.

## Removed or kept out of the narration

- **REMOVED**: "63,000" as a fact. Kept only as labelled FOLKLORE and corrected on screen.
- **NOT USED**: the winch / motorised cart that lowered gurneys (Mental Floss only in these searches). Rails and cable appear in the set dressing but are not narrated.
- **NOT USED**: the reopening as Woodhaven Geriatric Center (1962; some sources say 1963) and its closure (1980 / 1981 / 1982, sources conflict; abuse/neglect allegations). Dates conflict and the abuse allegations need care; out of scope for 50 s.
- **NOT USED**: Room 502 (suicide legend: the brief's self-harm rule) and "the Creeper".
- **NOT USED**: "the tunnel served as an air-raid shelter in WWII" (single unsourced listicle).
- **NOT USED**: the boy's name "Timmy" and any floor for him (sources conflict).
- **UNCERTAIN, worded around**: exact tunnel length (485 vs 500 ft) → "about five hundred feet".
