## Priority check: is the Carl "Charles" Webb identification official?

**NO — not officially confirmed as of the latest reporting found (mid/late 2026).**

- July 2022: Prof. Derek Abbott (University of Adelaide) and US genetic genealogist Colleen Fitzpatrick announced, from DNA in hairs caught in the plaster death mask/bust, that the man was Carl "Charles" Webb, an electrical engineer (also described as instrument maker) born 1905 in Footscray, Victoria. Abbott called the match very high-confidence. Sources: InDaily https://indaily.com.au/news/local/2022/07/27/somerton-mans-identity-revealed-professor-says ; Smithsonian https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/have-scholars-finally-identified-the-mysterious-somerton-man-180980540/ ; UPI https://www.upi.com/Top_News/World-News/2022/07/27/Identity-mystery-of-Australias-Somerton-Man-solved-with-DNA-after-74-years/6121658939367/ ; AP via WSB-TV https://www.wsbtv.com/news/trending/dna-leads-id-australias-somerton-man-researchers-say/HUGQRNBPFJEJHDDOWEITFHH2TA/
- 2022: SA Police and Forensic Science SA said they could not yet confirm the findings; identification is a matter for the State Coroner. Sources: AP/WSB-TV (above); Smithsonian (above).
- **6 July 2026, ABC News:** SA Police say their report to the coroner is close to complete; spokesperson: the coronial investigation is "complex and ongoing". https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-07-06/sa-police-report-coroner-into-somerton-man-case-very-soon/106869302 ; syndicated: https://www.yourlifechoices.com.au/crime/sa-police-report-to-coroner-into-somerton-man-case-complete-very-soon/
