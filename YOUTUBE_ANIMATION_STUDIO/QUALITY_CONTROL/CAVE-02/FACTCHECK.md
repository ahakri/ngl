## Removed / not used
- Concept hook "none of them could dive" was kept as "had ever dived". "Most couldn't swim" was dropped (single source).
- "Hours of diving" (concept). Durations vary by source: the first group came out in under 4 h per the Bangkok Post, and the whole operation on 8 July took ~11 h. Not narrated.
- Saman Kunan's age (37/38), the "air pipe" variant and the time of death: inconsistent across outlets, so not narrated.
- Saman Kunan statue/memorial and royal funeral: not narrated. The statue was not verified (search budget exhausted). The royal funeral has one snippet (Bangkok Post).
- "More than 10,000 people / 90 divers": 90 divers (50 foreign) appears in one snippet only (Gulf News), and 10,000 was not checked. Neither is narrated.
- Boys' names, ages and the coach's name: not used (minors; not needed).
- Dr Richard Harris and "ketamine": accurate (B18, B19) but not narrated, to keep the length down. They can go in the pinned comment.
- Beirut Pakbara (2019 death): single snippet, not narrated. This is also why the script avoids "the only rescuer to die".
- No invented dialogue. "How many of you?" / "Thirteen" is recorded speech.

## Visual ethics check
- The boys and coach are faceless silhouettes. There are no likenesses and no individual identification.
- Saman Kunan appears only as a distant silhouette from behind (S11). His death is shown by a torch glow fading and an empty line (S12–S13). No figure in distress and no body.
- Memorial card "IN MEMORY · Saman Kunan · Former Thai Navy SEAL" (S13) and closing text "IN MEMORY OF SAMAN KUNAN" (S19). The torch-on-a-rock image in S19 is a generic tribute image, not a depiction of a real memorial.
- No real footage or photos. The SEAL video is described, not recreated literally.
