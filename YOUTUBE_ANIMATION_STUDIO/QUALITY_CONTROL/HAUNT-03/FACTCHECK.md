## Removed or kept out of the narration

- **REMOVED**: "drivers report hitting a woman who isn't there" (concept beat). A targeted search found no corroborating source; the legend's hit-and-run element is about Mary's supposed death, not documented driver reports. Not used.
- **REMOVED**: "she points at the cemetery gates" (concept hook). Not found in any source; replaced by the sourced "asked to get out".
- **NOT USED**: the legend that Palus visited the Damen Avenue house next day and learned Mary was dead / saw her photo. Only the visit itself appears in snippets; the outcome was not verified with 2 sources.
- **CUT FOR LENGTH**: "He told it on camera in 1986" (VERIFIED, R1 + R7: 1986 videotaped interview used in the 1994 *Unsolved Mysteries* segment). Can be restored if the render runs short.
- **NOT USED**: candidate names (Bregovy, Norkus, Petkiewicz) and their deaths. Real deceased young women, details conflict; no benefit to naming them.
- **NOT USED**: "cold lips / icy touch", "white dress and dancing shoes" burial detail, 1973 Harlow's / Chet's Melody Lounge sightings: anecdotal, not checked to 2 sources this session.
- **NOT USED**: Willowbrook Ballroom fire (2016); seen in one snippet summary only, and not needed.
- **NOT USED**: scorched-handprint and lab-analysis rumours; kept to "marks like handprints".
