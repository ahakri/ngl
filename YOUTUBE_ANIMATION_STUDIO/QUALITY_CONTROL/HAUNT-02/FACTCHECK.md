## Removed or kept out of the narration

- **REMOVED from narration**: Kubrick renaming the room to 237 (true: H18, Timberline Lodge's request, plus the Scripps and Westword coverage). It was cut for runtime only and is kept for the description and packaging. Note: the Timberline request concerned Timberline's own room 217, not the Stanley's.
- **REMOVED**: the concept's line "the staff say the maid never really left" as a statement of fact. It is now explicitly FOLKLORE (L10).
- **REMOVED**: "63 years earlier" (concept hook). The arithmetic is fine, but it implied the blast killed someone in the room King slept in.
- **NOT USED**: "Presidential Suite" (H4) and "Stephen King Suite". The naming is inconsistent; the official page says only that 217 was once part of a larger suite until the mid-2000s.
- **NOT USED**: "snowed-in night" (H15 only), the son Joe being present (Fandango only), and "the reputation predates King" (H13 only; plausible but single-source).
- **NOT USED**: claims that unmarried couples feel a cold presence (tour lore, not needed), and the claim that Room 217 is "the most requested room" (that statement is Timberline's, about its own room 217; easy to confuse).
- **UNCERTAIN, labelled**: the 1911 details. The badge sub-text reads "1911 reports; details vary".
