## Build notes (new simple props)

- **Tape recorder**: dark-grey box 40x25x12 cm; two cylinders r=9 cm h=1 cm on top as reels (rotate ±1.5 rev/s); tape = thin plane between reels; red emissive sphere r=0.6 cm as REC lamp.
- **Bedroom**: `room` 3.2x3.6 m, door + sash window; two `bed` props, one `chair`, bedside lamp (cylinder + cone shade + point light), small chest of drawers (box). Floral wallpaper = procedural noise colour ramp; brown carpet.
- **Street**: `house_exterior` x3 in a row, flat brick fronts, low brick front walls (boxes), one sodium `point` light on a pole; wet road = `road` with high-gloss material.
- **Telephone**: box base + capsule handset; **evidence board**: plane with cork noise texture + `card` overlays.
- **Waveform**: 2D overlay (procedural noise line), NOT derived from the real recording.
- **Wrong detail continuity**: chair position A (40 cm out, angled to the left bed) in S01, S14, S15; position B (against wall) in S13. S07 has it toppled.

## Timing checks

- Line 1 starts at 0.0 s; 'tape recorder' at ~1.5 s. New information every 3–5 s through L13. Pattern interrupts: S06 (macro), S08 (graphic), S10–S12 (evidence board / document).
- Shot S08 (≈5 s) has the waveform scroll + zoom as its internal motion; S06 has an orbit. No static hold > 4 s except the deliberate dead-air hold at the end of S13.
