Timings are from a Kokoro test render (am_onyx, speed 1.06): total narration **67.45 s**. Shot boundaries = start of the shot's first line (pipeline `timing()` rule). 9:16, 1080x1920, 24 fps. Captions burned in (red accent, `emphasis` words highlighted) — keep overlays out of the bottom 20 % and right 15 %.

No real photos, no faces, no bodies or injuries shown anywhere. Human figures are faceless `char_figure` silhouettes only.
