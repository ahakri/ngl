## Build notes (new simple props)

- **Body-chute tunnel**: one mesh, rectangular tube 2.4x2.6 m with a shallow arched ceiling (subdivided top face, z-offset by cos), 60 m long, rotated -12 degrees about X; floor split into steps (array of 0.3x0.18 m boxes, left half) and ramp (right half) with two rail boxes 3x5 cm and a cable (thin cylinder). Concrete = noise-mixed grey with darker water-stain bands. Cage lamps: emissive sphere r=5 cm + point light 40 W every 6 m; one dead lamp (emission 0) near the far end in S01/S14.
- **Gurney**: box frame 1.9x0.6x0.05 m at 0.8 m on four thin legs + caster cylinders; folded sheet = flat slab 0.6x0.4x0.04 m, off-white. Roll = keyframed location along the slope axis (ease-in).
- **Sanatorium facade**: box_mesh; per storey a parapet slab + 30 pier boxes leaving dark openings (back-faced with near-black material); 5 storeys x 3.6 m; wings rotated 30 degrees; central entrance block +1 storey. Hill = ground plane with displacement; bare trees via forest builder.
- **4th-floor corridor**: `corridor` builder 3x40 m; replace the right wall with `wall_with_openings` (window bands, no glass) for moonlight stripes (sun light through openings + fog_volume).
- **Ball**: UV sphere r=3.5 cm, red rubber (roughness 0.6). S13 roll = keyframed location + rotation (rotation = distance / r).
- **Shadow figure**: `figure()` near-black, height 1.85 m; walk across a doorway in 24 frames.
- **Continuity**: ball present in S08, absent in S12, rolls in on S13. Gurney at top in S01 start and S14.
