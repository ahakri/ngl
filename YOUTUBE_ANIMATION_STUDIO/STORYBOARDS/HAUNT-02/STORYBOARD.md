## Build notes (new simple props)

- **Hotel facade**: `house_exterior` variant scaled to a long 4-storey block (x4 width), white walls, red roof, central cupola (cylinder + cone), rows of windows; `mountain_snow`-style backdrop ridges in `fog_volume`.
- **Corridor**: `corridor` builder with paired doors, brass sconces, dark-red patterned carpet; door 217 with brass `text3d` numbers.
- **Room 217**: `room` with tall window, brass `bed`, wardrobe (box), desk + `chair`, typewriter (box + cylinder roller + wedge key bank), suitcase (box with torus handle) on a luggage rack (two thin boxes). State A: suitcase closed (S09). State B: suitcase open and empty, 4 folded clothing squares (flattened boxes) on the bed, shoes (two capsules) paired under it (S15).
- **Gas lamp**: small brass bracket + glass sphere; heat-shimmer = refractive plane with animated noise. Explosion = white flash overlay + `particle_cloud` dust + boolean hole in corridor floor; no person in frame.
- **Fire hose**: tube along a curved path (cave_tunnel path logic at small radius), canvas material, brass cone nozzle.
- **Tour group**: 5 `char_figure` (coats) with `anim_walk`; lantern = point light in a small box frame.

## Timing checks

- Line 1 starts at 0.0 s; 'Room 217' lands at ~2.5 s (King's name at ~1.3 s). New information every 3–5 s. Pattern interrupts: S05 sepia flashback, S08 flash, S11 evidence board, S14 tour group.
- The dead-air beat is the 0.9 s pause after L14 at the end of S14 (empty corridor, total silence); the reveal is the hard cut into S15.
