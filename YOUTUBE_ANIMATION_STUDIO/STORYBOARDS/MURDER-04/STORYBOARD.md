# MURDER-04: Storyboard

The Zodiac "340" cipher (mailed Nov 1969), solved Dec 2020 by David Oranchak, Sam Blake and Jarl Van Eycke. Series: *Cold File*.

Beat map:
- cold open 0–6.5 s
- stakes and respect beat 6.5–18 s
- the solvers 18–28 s
- method turn 28.4 s ("not left to right")
- **mid-point turn 42.3 s ("Then words surfaced"): unreadable becomes readable**
- FBI acknowledgement 45 s
- twist and decoded taunt 48–59 s
- anticlimax 59.5 s
- open threads: Z13 and Z32, 65.5–73.5 s
- comment prompt and visual loop 73.6–75.8 s, back to frame 1

Timings come from a Kokoro test render (am_michael, speed 1.08): total narration **75.8 s**. Shot boundaries are the start of each shot's first line. 9:16, 1080x1920, 24 fps. Captions are burned in (red accent).

**Reusable prop: CIPHER GRID** (S01, S02, S10, S11, S16, S20, S22, S24; defocused behind S05)
- A 20x17 plane of individually keyframable text3d glyphs on aged paper.
- The glyphs are **stand-ins** (DejaVu/Oswald capitals, some mirrored or rotated, a few Greek letters, dots, slashes and triangles). They are not a symbol-for-symbol reproduction of the 1969 sheet, and the description and on-screen text never claim they are.
- Decoded English text on screen is the published plaintext, but only its first two non-graphic lines.
- "Letters rearranging" (S11) is a keyframed hop path: down 1 row, right 2 columns, wrapping at the edge. Visited glyphs lift and slide into a re-ordered strip.

**Modern screens** (S06, S12, S13, S14): a box monitor with an emissive screen texture (scrolling letter grid, mosaic of thumbnails, or a split-flap decode), the room otherwise black, with monitor glow as the key light.

**1969 sets** (S03 newsroom; S18 and S19 box TV): room builder, box typewriters, cone lamps, and a wood-veneer box TV with static.

No crime scenes, victims, weapons, handwriting or real envelopes. Solvers are faceless silhouettes; no likenesses. No suspect is named or depicted.


## S01  ·  0.0–4.8 s  (4.8 s)  ·  transition in: cut

- **Narration:** L01 "The Zodiac Killer mailed this. For fifty-one years, nobody could read it."
- **Visual:** CIPHER GRID PROP (build once, reuse): 20 rows x 17 columns = 340 glyph cells laid out on a flat plane, each cell a text3d glyph (extrude 0.01) in dark ink on an aged off-white paper plane. Glyphs are STAND-INS drawn from DejaVu/Oswald (Latin capitals, some mirrored or rotated 90/180 deg, a few Greek letters, dots, slashes, triangles) arranged so the page reads like the 1969 sheet without claiming to reproduce it symbol-for-symbol. Every glyph is its own object so it can be keyframed (emission, colour, position). Frame 1: extreme close-up already drifting across rows of glyphs on the paper; by 1.5 s the camera has pulled back to reveal the whole 20x17 block floating in black; cells flicker faint red as if being scanned.
