## Build notes (new simple props)

- **Reusable helpers** (PIPELINE/blender/lib_haunted.py): `figure(dress=True, cap=...)` for silhouettes, `mat_brass` for the lens barrel, `mat_cork` + `mat_paper` for board/document, `box_mesh` for steps/panels, `key_energy` for the flash spike, `flicker_mat_with_light` for the candle, `dust`.
- **Staircase**: 18 `box` steps (1.6 x 0.3 x 0.18 m) to a half-landing (box 1.6 x 1.6), return flight of 10; balusters = cylinders r 2.5 cm with two torus rings (turned look) every 0.25 m; square newel posts (box 0.2 m) with sphere finials; handrail = bevelled box along each flight; `bevel` all. Dark oak = `mat_noise_surface` brown, rough 0.5.
- **Panelled walls**: `room` walls with a grid of inset panels (boxes 0.6 x 0.9 x 0.02 m, darker frame material) up to 2.4 m; plain plaster above. Landing window: tall plane with lattice emission texture (cool grey).
- **Translucent figure**: `char_figure` height 1.7, coat=True (gown), hood/veil = cone + sphere cap; mix shader emission 0.4 + transparent (alpha 0.25) + small volume scatter; no face. Positions: landing (S01/S14 end), forming on upper flight (S06), 3 steps from bottom (S14 reveal).
- **Plate camera + tripod**: camera body box 25x25x20 cm (dark wood), bellows = tapered box (pleats via array of thin boxes), brass lens cylinder r 4 cm with removable cap (cylinder), ground-glass back plane; tripod = 3 long thin boxes (wood) splayed. **Flash gun**: cone dish reflector r 10 cm (chrome) + cylinder handle; flash = area light spike.
- **Darkroom**: red `area` light; tray = shallow box with liquid plane (glossy, noise ripple); plate = thin glass box with our own negative-tone render of the S01 staircase as an image texture (rendered first, inverted in PIL).
- **Lightbox (S10)**: box with emissive top; magnifier = torus + glass disc + cylinder handle.
- **Manor exterior**: `house_exterior` variant, wide 3-storey symmetrical, triangular-prism pediment, sash windows; generic (no replica).
- **Continuity**: figure on landing S01; absent S05, S13; forming S06; close S14 reveal, then back to landing for the loop.
