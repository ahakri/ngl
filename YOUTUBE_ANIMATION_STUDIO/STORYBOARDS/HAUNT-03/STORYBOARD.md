## Build notes (new simple props)

- **Reusable helpers** (PIPELINE/blender/lib_haunted.py): `figure(dress=True)` for the pale woman, `mat_wet_road` for the asphalt, `mat_cork` + `mat_paper` for the evidence board, `box_mesh` for car/piers, `key_energy` for headlight/police flicker, `room_fog`/`dust`.
- **Road**: `road` (length 200, width 7) with wet high-gloss material; `forest` (area 60, count 70, road_halfwidth 5) of bare `tree`s; `fog_volume` low and long (80x200x2.5 m, density 0.05). Headlights = two `spot` lights parented to the camera rig.
- **Pale woman**: `char_figure` height 1.62, coat=True (long dress), colour (0.75,0.75,0.72), emit 0.15; NO face geometry. In S14 render at 35% alpha (holdout mix).
- **Dance hall**: `house_exterior` variant w=22, d=12, floors=1, roof_h 2.0; six tall arched windows (box cut + half-cylinder top) with window_glow amber; canopy = thin box on two cylinders; `text3d` 'BALLROOM' on roof (generic).
- **Cemetery gates**: two box piers 0.9x0.9x3.2 m + sphere finials; 22 `cylinder` bars (r 0.012, h 2.6) with cone tips, two box rails; low stone wall boxes; headstone boxes (bevelled) beyond. Bent-bar state: SimpleDeform BEND (30 deg) on the two centre bars + dark palm-shaped decal at 1.1 m.
- **Car**: 1930s silhouette = box body + rounded box cabin (bevel) + 4 cylinder wheels + 2 emissive headlamp spheres + red emissive tail lamps. **Interior**: dashboard box, two dial cylinders (amber emission), torus wheel, seat boxes; window plane with fog/condensation noise.
- **Police car (S09)**: box body + light bar with alternating red/blue `point` lights via `flicker` (rate high, depth 1.0).
- **Globe (S12)**: UV sphere with wireframe modifier, slow rotation.
- **Continuity**: bars straight in S04/S07/S08; bent in S10/S14. Figure on verge S01/S15; behind bars S09; faint S14.
