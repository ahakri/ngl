## Build notes (new simple props)

- **Headstones**: ~25 box/slab primitives (0.5-1.0 m tall, 0.08-0.25 m thick), random tilt 5-15 degrees, 3 split into two boxes with a jagged boolean, 1 lying flat; weathered stone = noise grey + moss tint (in IR grade moss reads white).
- **Hero stone**: box 0.9x0.25x0.75 m, front face with an 8x6 checker texture (alternate tiles 3 mm recessed via displacement or two-tone stone). No name, no dates.
- **Infrared grade**: compositor: desaturate, curves lifting greens (grass/trees material with high-green albedo -> near-white), sky crushed to black, glare node (fog glow, small) for halation, heavy grain.
- **Farmhouse (S04)**: `house_exterior` scaled to one storey (gable, porch = box + 4 posts, porch light). Keyframe scale 1.0 -> 0.6 and alpha 1 -> 0 while the camera pushes forward (the 'shrinks as you approach' folklore).
- **Orbs**: 3 emissive spheres r=6 cm + 5 W point lights, slow noise paths.
- **SLR (S06)**: box 14x9x5 cm, cylinder lens r=3 cm, wedge pentaprism; film canister cylinder 3.2x5 cm with plain 'IR' decal.
- **Darkroom (S08)**: `room` 2x2.5 m, bench box, three flat tray boxes, red point light; print = plane with a blurred noise texture only (never an image of the figure).
- **Seated figure (S13)**: `figure(dress=True)` in a seated pose (hips 90 degrees, knees 90 degrees, hands in lap), faceless head, material = white emission 0.2 + transparency 0.5; fade-in via alpha keyframes over 24 frames. Head turned 20 degrees away from camera. Deliberately generic: not modelled on the 1991 image.
- **Continuity**: S01, S12 and S14 share framing; S13 is S12's framing with the figure added. The flattened grass patch is in S01, S12, S13 and S14.
